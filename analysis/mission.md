# IFANCA's mission, and the opportunities it opens

A one-page summary for the training session. Every quote below is copied word for word from ifanca.org, with the page it came from. The full list is in `analysis/claims.json`, the notes are in `analysis/coverage.csv`, and the picture is `viz/opportunity-map.html`.

Copy of the website made on 2026-10-04.

## How we read it

1. Claude read three pages: the homepage, About, and Beyond Certification.
2. It copied every promise IFANCA makes, word for word. A script checked that each one appears exactly on the page. It found 42.
3. It sorted the promises into the three pillars IFANCA names itself.
4. For each promise, it asked: can a consumer easily reach content about this online today?

## The three pillars

**Certification: 26 promises**
- In IFANCA's words: "We help companies create halal products that consumers can trust"
- Online and easy to find: 7. Online, with room to grow: 18. New opportunity: 1.

**Education: 15 promises**
- In IFANCA's words: "We are a resource to everyone looking to learn about halal."
- Online and easy to find: 1. Online, with room to grow: 13. New opportunity: 1.

**Institutions: 14 promises**
- In IFANCA's words: "We strive to best serve humanity by promoting food and health security and nutrition equity through local and global partnerships"
- Online and easy to find: 1. Online, with room to grow: 13.

A promise can belong to more than one pillar, so it counts once in each.

## What the three labels mean

- **Online and easy to find:** a consumer can click through to current content about it.
- **Online, with room to grow:** some content exists, but it is older, partial, or hard to reach.
- **New opportunity:** there is no content about it online yet.

## What this tells us

- **Certification is IFANCA's strongest pillar online.** The certification process, the list of 11,642 certified products, and IFANCA's recognitions are all easy to reach.
- **Education has the most room to grow.** IFANCA has a large library, but a consumer has no simple place to start learning what halal means.
- **Institutions work could be far more visible.** Programs, partnerships, and grants are reachable today only through the footer's Sitemap page.

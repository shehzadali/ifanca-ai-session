Back to All Skinny French Vanilla Iced Coffee Naazish YarKhan March 31, 2012 Copied! Glass size: 16 oz. Ingredients ice ¾ oz. Monin Sugar Free French Vanilla Syrup 3 oz. chilled strong coffee 3 oz. cold, low fat milk Step 1 Fill glass with ice. Step 2 Add remaining ingredients. Step 3 Cap and shake vigorously, or transfer from serving glass to other glass and back. Step 4 Garnish. Fewer than 50 calories.

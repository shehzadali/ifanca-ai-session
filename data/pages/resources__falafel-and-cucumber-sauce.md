Ingredients 1 can chickpeas (garbanzo beans), drained 1 onion, chopped ½ cup fresh parsley 2 cloves garlic, chopped 2 teaspoons ground cumin 1 teaspoon ground coriander 1 teaspoon salt 1 dash pepper 1 tablespoon olive oil oil for frying Sauce 1 (6 ounce) container plain yogurt ½ cucumber chopped 1 teaspoon dried dill weed salt and pepper to taste 1 tablespoon mayonnaise Directions In a large bowl combine chickpeas, onion, parsley, garlic, cumin, coriander, salt, olive oil and pepper until thick mix with hand blender. Make 8 balls and then flatten into patties. Heat 1 inch of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry patties in hot oil until brown on both sides. In a small bowl combine yogurt, cucumber, dill, salt, pepper and mayonnaise. Chill for at least 30 minutes. Serve with falafel.

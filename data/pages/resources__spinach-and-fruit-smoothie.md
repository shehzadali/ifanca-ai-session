Back to All Spinach and Fruit Smoothie Daphne Paras December 31, 2017 Copied! Servings: 1 Ingredients 2 tablespoons halal protein powder 8 strawberries 1 banana 1 cup fresh spinach 1 tablespoon hemp or chia seed 1 tablespoon coconut butter (optional) Step 1 Place all ingredients in blender. Step 2 Blend on high speed until smooth. Step 3 Enjoy.

Back to All Cream Cheese with Cucumber, Nectarine, and Kiwi Salsa YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD March 31, 2021 Copied! Servings: 12 Ingredients 1 nectarine or peach, chopped 1 kiwi, peeled and chopped 1 small cucumber, chopped 1 jalapeño pepper, finely chopped 8 ounces halal cream cheese 2 tablespoons onions, finely chopped 2 tablespoons cilantro or parsley ½ cup halal zesty Italian dressing* round, buttery crackers or pita chips 1 lime (optional) Step 1 Spread the cream cheese onto the bottom of a 9-inch pie plate. Step 2 Combine all ingredients except the pita chips or crackers, and spread over the cream cheese. Step 3 Serve with pita chips or crackers. Step 4 Add flavor by squeezing a lime over the dip before serving (optional). *The Italian dressing can be made in advance. Keep it stored in an airtight container and chilled.

Back to All Coconut Brownie Bites Omolara Funmilayo Recipe December 31, 2023 Copied! Servings: 14 Time: 15 minutes Ingredients 1 cup almonds 1/4 cup cocoa powder 1/2 cup unsweetened coconut flakes, divided 1 cup pitted dates, soaked and drained 1 1/2 tablespoons coconut oil 1 tablespoon raw honey Step 1 Combine the almonds, cocoa powder, and half of the shredded coconut in a food processor. Process into a fine powder. Step 2 Add in the soaked dates, coconut oil, and honey. Pulse until a dough-like consistency forms. Step 3 Roll dough into small bite-sized balls. Roll the balls in the remaining coconut flakes to coat. Notes Store in the fridge for up to 5 days or in the freezer for up to 3 months.

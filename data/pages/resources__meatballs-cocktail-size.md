Back to All Meatballs—Cocktail Size Chef Demetrios Haralambatos September 30, 2020 Copied! Ingredients 1 pound halal ground beef ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon ½ teaspoon ground cumin 1 large onion, chopped finely 1 tablespoon parsley ½ teaspoon mint, crushed 1 garlic clove, minced 2 eggs 1 slice of bread, soaked and squeezed dry salt and pepper to taste flour vegetable oil Step 1 Combine all the ingredients, except for the flour and oil, in a large bowl. Step 2 Roll the mixture into small balls (a little smaller than a walnut). Step 3 Dust with flour. Step 4 Heat oil in a sauté pan. Step 5 Fry the meatballs until cooked throughout. Variations Serve warm and without sauce. Add to your favorite tomato sauce. You may substitute 1 pound halal ground beef with a mixture of ½ pound halal ground lamb and ½ pound halal ground beef.

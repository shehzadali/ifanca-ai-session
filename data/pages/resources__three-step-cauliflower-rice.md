Back to All Three-Step Cauliflower “Rice” Amani Jabbar, MA September 30, 2014 Copied! Ingredients 1 medium head cauliflower 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil 1 clove garlic minced seasonings of choice Step 1 Remove outer leaves and stems from cauliflower. Step 2 Pulse florets of cauliflower in the food processor until they are the consistency of rice. Step 3 Heat oil in a large frying pan or wok over medium-high heat. Add garlic, riced cauliflower, and seasonings of choice. Toss until lightly browned and fragrant. Step 4 Serve with your favorite sauce.

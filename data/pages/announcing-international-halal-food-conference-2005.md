IFANCA is co-sponsoring International Halal Food Conference 2005 “A Window To The Halal World”. Under the theme HALAL: A Symbol Of Quality And Purity, the conference will be held on September 8-9, 2005, in Durban, South Africa. The conference, which is being organized by the South African National Halal Authority (SANHA) and is being co-sponsored by the Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America (IFANCA), is supported by a number of Food Corporations and Government Agencies. International Food Conference 2005 will be held in conjunction with the World Food Council meeting (September 12-13) in Cape Town. Also planned are tours around South Africa including sightseeing trips in and around Johannesburg, Durban and Cape Town as well as Kruger National Park and other tourist and safari attractions.

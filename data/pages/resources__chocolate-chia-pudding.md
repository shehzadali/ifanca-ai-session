Back to All Chocolate Chia Pudding Roni Enten Vissoker March 31, 2016 Copied! Servings: 1 Copyright © 2016 Roni Enten Vissoker Ingredients 1 cup unsweetened almond, coconut, or oat milk 2 tablespoons chia seeds 1 teaspoon raw cocoa powder ¼ teaspoon cinnamon ½ tablespoon maple syrup, or more to taste Step 1 Combine all ingredients in a bowl. Step 2 Refrigerate for at least 1 hour, stirring once an hour to keep the seeds from clumping. (Note: This makes a single serving, so you can scale this recipe up or down. It’s delicious on its own or topped with fresh fruit and/or toasted nuts.)

Back to All Pakistani Street-Style Bun Kabob Huma Ali Recipe March 31, 2026 Copied! Servings: 12-16 Time: 1 hour, 15 minutes Ingredients For kabob: 2 lbs halal beef 1 cup split chana dal, soaked 1 potato 1 tablespoon salt, or to taste 1 large onion, chopped Handful chopped cilantro Handful chopped mint 1 egg For chutney: 1 bunch cilantro 1 cup fresh mint 1 Serrano pepper 2 cloves garlic 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste 1-2 tablespoons tamarind pulp 1/2 teaspoon red chili flakes 1 teaspoon ground cumin For assembly: 12-16 dinner rolls or mini buns Thinly sliced onion Chopped cilantro Optional: fried egg, sliced cucumber Step 1 Wash the halal beef and place it in a pot with 1 liter of water, ginger-garlic paste, dried red chilies, salt, and potato. Boil until the meat is halfway cooked. Step 2 Add the soaked split chana dal and continue cooking until the meat and dal are fully cooked and the water has evaporated. Step 3 Let the mixture cool slightly, then grind it in a food processor until smooth. Step 4 Transfer to a bowl and mix in chopped onion, cilantro, and mint. Step 5 Shape the mixture into small patties. Step 6 Beat the egg in a bowl, dip each patty into the egg, and shallow fry in a pan until golden brown on both sides. Step 7 For the chutney, wash cilantro and mint. Combine all chutney ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth. Transfer to a bowl. Step 8 Warm the buns. To assemble, spread a generous layer of chutney on each bun, place a kabob on top, and add sliced onions and chopped cilantro. Optional: add a fried egg or cucumber slices. Serve warm.

Back to All Homemade Face Wash Stefanie Hays December 31, 2016 Copied! Ingredients ½ teaspoon sweet almond oil ½ cup castile soap 20 drops essential oil of choice ⅔ cups distilled water Step 1 Pour the sweet almond oil and castile soap into a foaming soap dispenser. Step 2 Add the essential oil; swirl to combine. Step 3 Fill the container with distilled water and screw on the top.

Back to All Nutty Yogurt Parfait Maryam Funmilayo March 31, 2022 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients ½ cup organic peanuts ½ cup organic tiger nuts ½ cup organic pistachios ½ cup organic walnuts ½ cup organic cashew nuts ½ cup organic pecans ½ cup organic almonds ½ cup organic shredded coconut 3 cups organic halal yogurt Step 1 Blend all the nuts (except the shredded coconut) for 30 seconds. Step 2 Add the shredded coconut and mix. Step 3 Pour the yogurt into a medium-sized bowl. Step 4 Sprinkle the nuts on top of the yogurt. Step 5 Serve chilled.

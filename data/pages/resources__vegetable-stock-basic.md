Back to All Vegetable Stock (Basic) Chef Demetrios Haralambatos December 31, 2019 Copied! Ingredients 1 tablespoon olive oil 1 large onion, chopped into 1 inch pieces 2 stalks celery, chopped into 1 inch pieces 2 large carrots, chopped into 1 inch pieces 1 bunch green onions, chopped 8 cloves garlic, minced 8 sprigs fresh parsley 6 sprigs fresh thyme 2 bay leaves 1 teaspoon salt 2 quarts water Step 1 Heat oil in your soup pot. Step 2 Add onion, celery, carrots, scallions, garlic, parsley, thyme, and bay leaves. Step 3 Cook over high heat for 5 to 10-minutes, stirring frequently. Step 4 Add salt and water, bring to a boil, then lower heat and simmer, uncovered, for 30-minutes. Step 5 Strain and discard the vegetables.

Back to All Open Face Crab Salad Sandwich YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD September 30, 2015 Copied! Servings: 1 Ingredients 1 ciabatta bread loaf, sliced in half 3 ounces imitation crab meat (pollack fish) 1 tablespoon low-fat honey mustard ½ teaspoon black pepper 1 tablespoon artichoke hummus ½ cup fresh baby spinach ½ cup alfalfa sprouts 1 ounce Cabot Pepper Jack Light Cheese Step 1 In a small mixing bowl, combine the crab meat with honey mustard and black pepper. Set aside. Step 2 Spread hummus on sliced bread. Step 3 Add fresh spinach and sprouts. Top with crab and sliced cheese.

Servings: 2 Prep Time: 10 minutes Ingredients 1 cup of old-fashioned rolled oats ½ cup Organic Valley Grassmilk® Milk ½ cup pumpkin puree 1 tablespoon pure maple syrup 1 tablespoon flax meal or chia seeds (or ½ tablespoon of each) 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice pinch of salt toasted pecans and dried cranberries for topping Step 1 In a bowl, whisk together rolled oats, Organic Valley Grassmilk®, pumpkin puree, pure maple syrup, flax meal or chia seeds, pumpkin pie spice, and pinch of salt. Step 2 Divide into two jars. Step 3 Seal jars and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight. Step 4 Add toppings just before eating.

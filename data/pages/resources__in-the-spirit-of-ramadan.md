Back to All In the Spirit of Ramadan Halal Consumer Magazine June 30, 2012 Copied! If it’s Ramadan, there have got to be dates in the house. Here are some delicious recipes for this healthy yet deliciously sweet fruit. IFANCA halal-certified date manufacturers include Bard Valley Medjool Date, Arizona, Desert Valley Date, California, Mariani Packing Company, California, and Sun Dates LLC, California. If dates are halal, why do we certify them, you’re wondering? Yes, dates are halal but how about the coating on them that keeps them from spoiling? Or the packing materials? IFANCA halal certification ensures that it’s all halal.

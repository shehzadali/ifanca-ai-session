Back to All Roasted Figs and Almonds Chef Demetrios Haralambatos June 30, 2020 Copied! Ingredients 12 figs, cut in half 6-8 tablespoons pomegranate juice 2 tablespoons Demerara (you may substitute light brown sugar) zest of 1 orange, finely grated ¼ cup blanched almonds ½ cup halal yogurt honey Step 1 Preheat oven to 400°F. Step 2 Place figs, cut side up, on a cookie sheet, pour pomegranate juice over each, and sprinkle Demerara over the tops. Step 3 Sprinkle the orange zest, then place a few larger almonds on each fig, and sprinkle the balance of the almonds around them. Step 4 Place in oven for 10-12 minutes, until soft. Serve hot, with yogurt and honey.

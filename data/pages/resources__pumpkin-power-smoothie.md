Back to All Pumpkin Power Smoothie Saira Mohiuddin September 30, 2016 Copied! Servings: 1 Ingredients 2 tablespoons pumpkin puree ¼ cup Cabot Greek-Style Yogurt 1½ cups Organic Valley® Organic Lowfat 1% Milk 1 scoop Abbott EAS Complete Protein Powder Nutrition Shake Mix Vanilla (optional) ¼ teaspoon cinnamon handful of ice Step 1 Blend all ingredients for 30 seconds in a blender. Pour into glass and enjoy!

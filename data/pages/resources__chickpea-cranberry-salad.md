Back to All Chickpea Cranberry Salad Omolara Funmilayo June 30, 2022 Copied! Servings: 5 Time: 15 minutes Ingredients 1 medium apple, diced 2 cups cooked chickpeas, rinsed and drained ½ cup unsweetened dried cranberries 4 cups napa cabbage, finely sliced ¼ cup pumpkin seeds ¼ cup red onion, diced 1 teaspoon celery salt 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar 2 teaspoons avocado oil 1 tablespoon ground mustard (optional) Step 1 Combine all ingredients in a large bowl and toss until well combined. Step 2 Divide between plates and enjoy! Note: Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 4 days. One serving is approximately 1½ cups.

Back to All Mexican Hot Chocolate Ice Cream Annan Shehadi June 30, 2017 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 1 pint heavy whipping cream 1 (14-ounce) can sweetened condensed milk 4 tablespoons cocoa powder 1 teaspoon cinnamon ¼ teaspoon ground cayenne pepper Step 1 In a bowl, whip heavy cream until it thickens and peaks form, about 3 minutes. Step 2 In a separate bowl, mix sweetened condensed milk, cinnamon, cayenne, and sift the cocoa powder so as not to clump. Step 3 Fold condensed milk mixture into the whipped cream with a spatula. Mix well. Step 4 Pour into a container and freeze for at least 6 hours before eating. Step 5 Enjoy!

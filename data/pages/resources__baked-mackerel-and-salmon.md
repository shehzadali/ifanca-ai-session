Back to All Baked Mackerel and Salmon Maryam Funmilayo June 30, 2022 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 3 mackerel filets 3 salmon filets 6 medium organic lemons 1 teaspoon paprika 1 teaspoon sweet basil leaves 1 teaspoon thyme 1 teaspoon rosemary leaves 1 tablespoon curry powder 2 cups organic baby carrots, grated Step 1 Place the fish in a large bowl. Step 2 Squeeze the lemons over the fish. Step 3 Add the herbs and spices and let the fish marinate for an hour in the fridge. Step 4 After one hour, pour the grated carrots over the marinated fish and bake for 45 minutes at 350°F. Step 5 Serve with bulgur or quinoa or enjoy the dish alone!

Servings: 6 Ingredients 3 cups mushroom broth ½ cup extra virgin olive oil 3 heads/bulbs garlic, minced 1 small red onion, minced 8 medium-size tomatoes, minced 3 medium-size habanero peppers, minced 3 medium-size cayenne peppers, minced 1 medium-size raw ginger, minced 2 tablespoons turmeric powder 1 tablespoon salt 1 teaspoon black pepper powder 2 tablespoons whole black peppercorns 1 teaspoon coriander 1 teaspoon of Italian seasoning Step 1 In a large pot, pour the mushroom broth and olive oil and let the mixture boil for 5 minutes. Step 2 Add in the garlic, onion, tomatoes, peppers, and ginger. Let the mixture boil for another 10 minutes. Step 3 Add in the salt and all the spices and herbs. Let the mixture boil for another 5 minutes. Step 4 Serve with baked or cooked potatoes or cooked yam.

Back to All Oven Roasted Potato Wedges Tayyaba Syed June 30, 2013 Copied! Serving Size: 4 | Total Prep Time: 40 minutes Ingredients 2 medium potatoes, scrubbed and rinsed 1½ tablespoons olive oil 2 garlic cloves, minced/mashed ¼ teaspoon dried rosemary ¼ teaspoon dried oregano ¼ teaspoon dried thyme ¼ teaspoon paprika ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper ½ teaspoon salt Step 1 Preheat oven to 425 degrees F. Step 2 Wash and cut potatoes into wedges. Step 3 Mix all spices with oil in a mixing bowl. Step 4 Toss/coat the potatoes with the spice mix. Step 5 Bake evenly for 35 minutes in the oven.

Back to All Triple Berry Smoothie Dr. Azeem Haleem December 31, 2016 Copied! Servings: 1 Ingredients 1 banana 1 kiwi ½ apple 1 cup black seedless grapes 3 tablespoons organic honey 1 teaspoon of turmeric powder 1 tablespoon kalonji 3 tablespoons Cabot Greek-Style Yogurt 2 tablespoons walnuts frozen triple berry mix frozen blueberries frozen strawberries 100% grape juice (no sugar added) Step 1 Blend all ingredients together and drink!

IFANCA takes this opportunity to wish everyone Ramadan Mubarak. May the blessed month of Ramadan bring you inner peace and spiritual fulfillment. May ALLAH, Subhanahu wa ta’ala, accept your fasting and night salat and reward you with Jannah. Ramadan is the 9th month in the Islamic calendar, known as the Hijra calendar. During this month, all able Muslims must refrain from eating and drinking from before dawn until sunset, every day of the month. Other restrictions also apply. Muslims are encouraged to spend more time in prayer and contemplation; remembering the LORD; reciting the Quran and performing more good deeds. Additional prayers are observed every night of the month. For more information on Ramadan, contact your local Islamic Center or Masjid (Mosque).

Back to All Egg Salad Alia Shalabi March 31, 2017 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 8 large hard-boiled eggs 2 tablespoons mayonnaise 1 teaspoon yellow mustard ¾ teaspoon salt ½ teaspoon pepper ¼ teaspoon paprika Step 1 Chop up the hard-boiled eggs. Step 2 Add the remaining ingredients and combine well. Step 3 Serve on bread, toast, or with crackers. Enjoy!

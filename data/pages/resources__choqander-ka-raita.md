Back to All Choqander Ka Raita Zainab Razavi Recipe June 30, 2024 Copied! Servings: 4-6 Time: 20 minutes Ingredients 1 ½ cups whole milk yogurt 3 tablespoons sour cream ½ cup water (adjust according to preference) 1 small beetroot (boiled, skin removed, and shredded) 1 Persian cucumber, shredded ½ purple onion, shredded ½ teaspoon salt ½ teaspoon chaat masala ¼ teaspoon black pepper ¼ teaspoon garlic powder ½ teaspoon dried mint leaves ½ small green chili, de-seeded and finely chopped Baghar (Tempering): 2 tablespoons oil ¼ teaspoon cumin seeds ½ teaspoon mustard seeds 1 teaspoon urad daal 2 garlic cloves, sliced 4-5 curry leaves Step 1 In a mixing bowl, combine the shredded beetroot, cucumber, onions, yogurt, sour cream, and water. Stir until well mixed. Step 2 Add the salt, chaat masala, black pepper, garlic powder, dried mint leaves, and green chilies to the yogurt mixture. Stir well to combine all the flavors. Step 3 For the baghaar, heat the oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds, mustard seeds, urad daal, sliced garlic, and curry leaves. Sauté until everything turns golden brown and starts to crackle. Step 4 Remove the baghaar from heat and allow it to cool slightly. Once cooled, drizzle the tempered oil and spices over the yogurt mixture. Step 5 Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve immediately!

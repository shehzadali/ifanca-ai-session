Back to All Mango Matcha Smoothie Saira Mohiuddin March 31, 2021 Copied! Servings: 1 Ingredients ¾ cup frozen organic mango 1 cup organic whole milk ½ scoop vegan vanilla protein ½ teaspoon matcha powder ¼ cup water Step 1 Add mango, milk, protein, and matcha powder to a blender, and blend. Step 2 Add the water sparingly while blending to help create a perfect smoothie finish without adding more calories to the drink. Step 3 Enjoy!

Back to All Grape Ambrosia YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD June 30, 2017 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 1 (15-ounce) can mandarin oranges 2 bananas, sliced 2 cups white grapes ½ cup Medjool dates, pitted, diced 4 tablespoons lemon juice ½ cup flaked coconut ½ pint whipping cream Step 1 Combine well-drained fruits and dates, sprinkle with lemon juice, chill. Step 2 Fold in whipped topping (or cream). Serve on crisp lettuce. Step 3 Sprinkle coconut on top. Step 4 Garnish with maraschino cherry. Step 5 If a tart salad is preferred, cut down on whipped topping and add ½ cup mayonnaise.

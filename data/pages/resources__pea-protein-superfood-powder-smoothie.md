Back to All Pea Protein Superfood Powder Smoothie YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad MS, RD, LD September 30, 2019 Copied! Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 cup cashew milk 1 cup spinach, fresh 1 medium banana, frozen ½ cup strawberries, frozen ¼ cup blueberries, frozen 1 scoop pea protein powder ¼ teaspoon vanilla bean powder 1 Natural Delight Medjool date for extra sweetness 1 cup ice Step 1 Combine ingredients in blender. Step 2 Blend until smooth and creamy. Step 3 Serve chilled.

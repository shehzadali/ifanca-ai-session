Back to All Dark Cocoa Powder and Cacao Nibs Superfood Powder Smoothie YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad MS, RD, LD September 30, 2019 Copied! Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 cup coconut milk 1 medium banana, frozen 2 tablespoons raw dark cocoa powder and cacao nibs 2 tablespoons peanut butter 1 dash of cinnamon 1 cup of ice Step 1 Combine ingredients in blender. Step 2 Blend until smooth and creamy. Step 3 Serve chilled.

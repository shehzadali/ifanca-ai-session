Ingredients 1 large onion, quartered 3 stalks celery, chopped into 1 inch pieces 1 large carrot, chopped into 1 inch pieces 1-pound halal chicken pieces 1½ teaspoons salt 3 whole cloves 6 cups water Step 1 Place onion, celery, carrot, chicken pieces, salt, and cloves in large soup pot. Step 2 Add 6 cups water and bring to a boil. Step 3 Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 1-hour. Step 4 Remove chicken and vegetables. Strain stock and skim fat off the surface.

Ingredients 1 (15 ounce) refrigerated piecrust ¾ cup SPLENDA No Calorie Sweetener, Granulated ¼ cup cornstarch 2 cups 2% reduced-fat milk ⅓ cup egg substitute 2 teaspoons imitation vanilla extract ⅛ teaspoon salt 1 tablespoon butter 1 teaspoon alcohol-free banana extract 1½ cups sliced bananas Garnish Halal fat-free whipped topping and finely chopped walnuts.

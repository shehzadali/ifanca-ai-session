This week, the Algerian government issued an update regarding the dairy products that require a halal certificate. As of now, milk powder no longer requires a halal certificate to be imported into Algeria. However, other dairy commodities still require halal certification from the Institut Musulman de la Grande Mosquée de Paris (IMMP) of the Grande Mosquée de Paris (GMP) or an agency approved by them. For more information, please see this article at Mintec .

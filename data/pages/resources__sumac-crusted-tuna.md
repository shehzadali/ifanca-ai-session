Back to All Sumac Crusted Tuna Chef Demetrios Haralambatos June 30, 2020 Copied! Ingredients 2 tuna steaks, about 6-ounce portions olive oil 1 tablespoon sumac Step 1 Brush the tuna with olive oil and rub the sumac on all sides. Step 2 Heat a skillet; when hot, place the tuna on it and cook for 2-3 minutes per side. Step 3 Remove from heat and serve immediately. You may serve this with cooked vegetable, or placed over a salad or rice. Salmon may be substituted for the tuna.

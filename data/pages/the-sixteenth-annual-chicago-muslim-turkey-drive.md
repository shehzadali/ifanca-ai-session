CHICAGO, Illinois (October 25, 2016) — We are pleased to announce the 16th edition of the Chicago Muslim Turkey Drive. The drive increases turnout at parent-teacher conferences while also providing Thanksgiving turkeys to families in need. This year’s goal is distribute 5,000 turkeys at eight (8) Chicago Public Schools! On November 22nd, we will be distributing turkeys at: Carnegie Elementary, 1414 E. 61st Place Dulles Elementary, 6311 S. Calumet Emmett Till Academy, 6543 S. Champlain Fairfield Academy, 6201 S. Fairfield Fiske Elementary, 6020 S. Langley South Shore Academy, 1415 E. 70th Street Wadsworth Elementary, 6650 S. Ellis Woodlawn Academy, 6657 S. Kimbark as well as at the Inner-city Muslim Action Network (IMAN). The Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America (IFANCA®) is again supporting the Turkey Drive through their Sabeel Food Pantry initiative. In fact this year, IFANCA will match every donation, dollar for dollar. Turkeys are $20 each and donations can be made online at www.sabeelpantry.org or by sending a check made payable to “Sabeel Food Pantry” to: Turkey Drive c/o Jihad Shoshara P O Box 535 La Grange, IL 60525 For more information on the Turkey Drive, please visit http://www.sabeelpantry.org .

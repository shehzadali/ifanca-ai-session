Back to All Upcoming IFANCA Activities Halal Consumer Magazine September 30, 2000 Copied! On October 30-31, IFANCA plans to be in Bangkok, Thailand for the HGH Back-up by Science conference. Dr. Chaudry will present a paper on some of the scientific evidence of Islamic guidance. On November 12-14, 2000, IFANCA plans to be in Buena Park, California, playing host to the Muslim community and food industry at the Halal Food Conference 2000. This is an excellent chance for Muslim consumers to meet decision makers in the food industry and exchange ideas and information on desired products. Halal Food Conference 2000 is sponsored by IFANCA and the Shura Council of Southern California. On March 14-18, 2001, IFANCA plans to be in Singapore for the 5th International Muslim Food and Technology Exhibition. Also in March, 2001, IFANCA plans to be in Jakarta, Indonesia, for the Jakarta Food Fair. And on May 15-17, 2001, IFANCA plans to be in Paris, France, as sponsor of the Halal Food Conference 2001. This is an excellent opportunity for those who cannot make the November conference to learn about the opportunities and requirements for certified Halal food production. Mark your calendars and plan to join us. These promise to be enlightening events.

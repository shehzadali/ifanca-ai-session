Back to All Pan Grilled Chicken Burger Aisha Kureishy June 30, 2013 Copied! Preparation Time: 5 minutes | Cooking Time: 15-20 minutes Ingredients 1 pound lean and thawed ground halal chicken meat salt to taste pepper to taste 1 teaspoon garlic powder 1 finely diced tomato 2 finely diced green onions 1 finely diced green pepper 3-4 teaspoons oil or oil spray can coriander or mint leaves for garnish Step 1 Mix all ingredients into the ground halal chicken. Step 2 Use a burger presser or make thin chicken patties. Step 3 Heat oil on medium heat, place 3-4 patties at a time, and turn when getting brown. Step 4 Serve them warm. Garnish with coriander or mint leaves.

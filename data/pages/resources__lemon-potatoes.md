Back to All Lemon Potatoes Chef Demetrios Haralambatos September 30, 2020 Copied! Ingredients 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled oregano, crushed salt olive oil juice of 2 lemons water additional olive oil for drizzling Step 1 Pre-heat oven to 400°F. Step 2 Peel and cut the potatoes into long wedges. Step 3 In an oven-safe baking dish, arrange the potato wedges and season with salt and oregano. Step 4 Drizzle with olive oil and add the lemon juice. Step 5 Pour enough water to cover the potatoes. Step 6 Bake until the water has been absorbed and/or evaporated. Step 7 Drizzle additional olive oil over the top of the potatoes and return to the oven to brown.

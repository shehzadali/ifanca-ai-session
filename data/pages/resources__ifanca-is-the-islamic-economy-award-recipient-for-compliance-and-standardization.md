Back to All IFANCA® Is the Islamic Economy Award Recipient for Compliance and Standardization December 31, 2013 Copied! IFANCA congratulates the winners of the Islamic Economy Awards. The awards “celebrated established and emerging companies that have demonstrated significant business and social impact using the Islamic economic ethos, and have inspired a new generation of leaders.” The integrity and credibility of the Award was managed independently by Thomson Reuters, the world’s leading information provider; audited by Deloitte, the world’s largest privately-held professional services organization; and adjudicated by an esteemed Judges’ panel based on formal, established criteria. A panel of eleven judges selected the award winners by first narrowing the nominees to three finalists in each category and then selecting the winner from among the three finalists.

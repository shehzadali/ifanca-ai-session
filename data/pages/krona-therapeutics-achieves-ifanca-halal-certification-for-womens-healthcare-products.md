CHICAGO, Illinois (February 4, 2019) – The Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America (IFANCA®) and Krona Therapeutics, Inc. are proud to announce the halal certification of Krona’s Natavi™ supplements for women. Halal certification was granted after a thorough review of the ingredients, production process, packaging, and logistics, and meeting with plant personnel demonstrated Krona’s commitment to adhering to the certification requirements. IFANCA halal-certified products qualify to display the IFANCA registered Crescent-M service mark, letting consumers know the product meets their Islamic dietary requirements. Krona desires to offer mothers the best opportunity to deliver healthy babies through its Natavi™ line of products. Natavi™ Prima provides nutrients for women who are planning to become pregnant, Natavi™ PNV provides nutrients while they are pregnant, and Natavi™ Lactant provides nutrients for breastfeeding women. Each of the three products is uniquely formulated to closely match the RDA requirements for each stage of early motherhood, containing the right amounts of such key ingredients as folic acid, iodine, DHA, EPA, and many others. All three products have been certified halal. “It is of utmost importance to Krona to offer to all women of childbearing age high quality products that help prevent abnormal brain development and other birth defects. This includes our commitment to Muslim mothers. The Halal certification process was very efficient and we are proud to have successfully completed it,” said Angel Biaggi, President of Krona. IFANCA President, Muhammad Munir Chaudry, PhD states: “We are delighted to help bring these products to the global community. Children are our future and these products should help meet the nutrition needs of their mothers, giving the newborns a healthier advantage.” Krona Krona Therapeutics, Inc. is devoted to their mission of bringing innovative solutions to women and the medical community that serves them. As a specialty pharmaceutical company, they retain a consulting board that includes pediatricians, ob-gyns, lactation specialists, and nutritionists. This team of experts gives the company unique and comprehensive insight into the health care needs of women. This information is vital in assisting Krona Therapeutics as they pursue their passion to create therapies that are affordable and vital in women’s health care. Their dedication to ongoing research and development reflects their desire to identify therapeutic needs, to increase the value of existing therapies, and to offer new and innovative therapeutic products. IFANCA® A non-profit headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, the Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America (IFANCA) is a global leader in halal certification and education. Featured in various media such as the Wall Street Journal , Prepared Foods , CNBC, and CNN, IFANCA has been promoting halal since 1982. IFANCA was awarded the “Best Halal Related Service Provider Award” by the Halal Journal at the World Halal Forum 2007 and the Islamic Economy Award for Compliance and Standardization by the Global Islamic Economy Summit (GIES®) 2013. IFANCA is endorsed by many countries and organizations including MUI (Indonesia), JAKIM (Malaysia), and MUIS (Singapore) and is accredited by GAC (GCC Accreditation Center) and EIAC (Emirates International Accreditation Centre), and IFANCA halal-certified products are sold in nearly every country of the world.

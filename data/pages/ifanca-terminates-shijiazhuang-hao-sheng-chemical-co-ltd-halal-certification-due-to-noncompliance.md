Shijiazhuang Hao Sheng Chemical Co Ltd is no longer halal certified by IFANCA. IFANCA halal certification was terminated as of November 7, 2013, due to noncompliance. Shijiazhuang Hao Sheng Chemical Co Ltd is no longer authorized to use the Crescent-M logo on their products and customers should be aware that production as of November 7, 2013, is not certified by IFANCA. Should you find any product displaying the IFANCA Crescent-M logo on it after this date, please notify IFANCA immediately.

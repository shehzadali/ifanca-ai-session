Prep Time: 15 minutes Cook Time: 1 hour Servings: 8-10 Ingredients 4 cups apples (peeled and chopped into small pieces) 2 cups sugar 2 cups flour 1½ teaspoons baking soda 1 teaspoon salt 2 teaspoons cinnamon 2 eggs ¾ cup oil 2 teaspoons halal vanilla flavor 1 cup chopped walnuts Step 1 Preheat oven to 350°F. Step 2 Oil 9×13-inch glass or other baking pan. Step 3 Mix all ingredients together in large bowl. Step 4 Pour batter into pan. Step 5 Bake for about 1 hour or until cake center is fully baked.

Back to All Persimmon Fruit Salad Maryam Funmilayo June 30, 2018 Copied! Serving size: 6 Ingredients 5 medium-sized persimmons 2 green apples, diced 1 cup of green grapes 2 small dates, chopped ¼ cup chopped pecans or walnuts Step 1 Cut the persimmons and apples in small dices, and shred the dates. Add the grapes and mix all the fruits together in a medium bowl. Step 2 Top with either pecans or walnuts. Step 3 Chill and serve!

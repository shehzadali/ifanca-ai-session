Back to All Vegetable Pizza Tayyaba Syed June 30, 2013 Copied! Preparation Time: 10 minutes | Cooking Time: 15-20 minutes Ingredients 4-5 non-pocket pita breads (the ones used in gyros sandwiches) ½ cup pasta sauce ½ cup baby spinach leaves 1 cup chopped bell peppers (red, orange, yellow) ½ red onion, sliced ½ cup chopped olives 6-8 cherry tomatoes sliced in half 1 cup halal feta cheese, crumbled Step 1 Preheat oven to 350 degrees F. Step 2 Arrange pita breads on baking sheets. Step 3 Spread two tablespoons of pasta sauce on each pita bread. Step 4 Place a few leaves of baby spinach on each pita. Step 5 Sprinkle each pita bread with a mix of all the chopped veggies. Step 6 Sprinkle feta cheese on top. Step 7 Bake for 10-12 minutes.

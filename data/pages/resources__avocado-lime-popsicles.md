Back to All Avocado Lime Popsicles Mujahed Khan September 30, 2013 Copied! Ingredients 2 ripened avocados, peeled and pitted 1 cup low fat milk ¼ cup sugar 2 tablespoons lime juice (from one large lime) ⅛ teaspoon salt Step 1 Put all ingredients in the blender and blend until smooth. Step 2 Pour into popsicle molds and freeze.

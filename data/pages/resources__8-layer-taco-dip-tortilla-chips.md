Back to All 8-Layer Taco Dip & Tortilla Chips Nadia Chisti June 30, 2009 Copied! Prep Time: 10 minutes Total Time: 3-4 hours Servings: 8 Ingredients 1 16 oz. can vegetarian refried beans 1 16 oz. tub sour cream 1 tsp. taco seasoning mix 1 16 oz. jar salsa 2 cups shredded cheese 2 cups shredded lettuce 1 cup diced tomatoes ¾ cup chopped green onions 1 2.25 oz can sliced black olives tortilla chips Step 1 In a bowl, mix 1/4 cup sour cream and refried beans. Step 2 Add taco seasoning into mixture. Step 3 In a large, deep serving dish, spread mixture as bottom layer of dip. Step 4 Spread remaining sour cream evenly over mixture. Step 5 Pour salsa as 3rd layer and spread. Step 6 Sprinkle shredded cheese evenly. Step 7 Next, cover with lettuce. Step 8 Spread tomatoes over the lettuce. Step 9 Add green onions as 7th layer. Step 10 Top off and decorate with desired amount of olives (drained). Step 11 Refrigerate for 3-4 hours. Step 12 Serve with tortilla chips or use as a filling inside a taco.

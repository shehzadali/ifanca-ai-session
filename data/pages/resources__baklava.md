Ingredients 1 cup unsalted butter 16 ounce package phyllo sheets 2 cups chopped pecans 1½ cups water ⅓ cup white sugar 1 cinnamon stick 1 cup honey Step 1 Melt the butter over low heat. Pour 2 tablespoons of the butter into the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking pan. Step 2 Layer 4-6 phyllo sheets in the pan. Sprinkle with ½ cup of pecans over the phyllo sheets, brush generously with the melted butter, and sprinkle with ½ cup of pecans. Continue layering until all is used. Step 3 With a sharp knife, cut phyllo sheet to form diamonds. Add remaining butter over the filling. Step 4 Bake 45 to 50 minutes in the preheated 350 degrees oven until golden brown. Step 5 Meanwhile, combine the sugar, water and cinnamon stick in a medium saucepan and bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Simmer for 10 minutes. Step 6 Mix in honey and remove from heat and discard cinnamon stick. Step 7 Pour honey mixture over hot baklava. Let it cool and serve warm.

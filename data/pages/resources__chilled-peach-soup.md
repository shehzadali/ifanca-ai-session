Back to All Chilled Peach Soup Chef Demetrios Haralambatos December 31, 2021 Copied! Ingredients ½ cup sugar 1 teaspoon fresh mint leaves, chopped ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon 2 cups half-and-half cream 10 fresh peaches, sliced Step 1 Place sugar, mint leaves, cinnamon, and half-and-half in a bowl. Stir until the sugar has dissolved and the mixture is fully combined. Step 2 Add sliced peaches and transfer to a saucepan. Step 3 Cook over medium heat for 15 minutes, stirring frequently. If necessary, lower the heat to prevent scorching the cream. Step 4 Cook over medium heat for 15 minutes, stirring frequently. If necessary, lower the heat to prevent scorching the cream. Step 5 Allow to cool, then transfer to a blender or food processor and blend until completely smooth. Step 6 Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve. Step 7 Serve chilled.

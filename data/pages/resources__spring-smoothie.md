Back to All Spring Smoothie YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD March 31, 2017 Copied! Servings: 2 Ingredients 2 cups Organic Valley milk 1 large banana 1 cup fresh mango, sliced 1 teaspoon cinnamon 1 teaspoon honey 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated 1 cup ice 1 fresh stalk of mint Step 1 Add all ingredients except mint to a blender and pulse until smooth and creamy. Step 2 Top with mint before serving.

Ingredients ¼ cup basmati rice 2 cups tomato puree, divided 1 teaspoon dried mint ½ teaspoon salt ⅛ teaspoon black pepper 2 pounds small zucchini ½ teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon minced garlic 1 tablespoon lemon juice Step 1 Mix together half boiled basmati rice, 2 tablespoons tomato puree, salt, and pepper. Step 2 Slice the ends off of the zucchini and hollow out the centers using an apple corer. Stuff with the above mixture. Step 3 Stir together the remaining tomato puree with ½ teaspoon salt in a large skillet. Place the stuffed zucchini into the sauce, and add enough water to cover the zucchini. Bring to a simmer for 30 minutes. Step 4 Stir in the garlic, lemon juice, and mint. Simmer for an additional 15 minutes. Serve cut up in halves or rounds.

February 23, 2026 10:28 AM CST CHICAGO, IL – IFANCA® (Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America) acknowledges the recent publication of the reciprocal trade agreement between the United States and Indonesia by the Office of the United States Trade Representative (USTR). We recognize that the document includes specific articles regarding halal certification for U.S. products. Understanding the importance of these updates, IFANCA® will be working closely with both the U.S. and Indonesian governments. Our goal is to secure comprehensive clarity to ensure that the implementation of this agreement is seamless, accurate, and efficient for all stakeholders. IFANCA® remains steadfast in its commitment to supporting the industry through the certification process, ensuring global consumers can continue to trust and enjoy the integrity of the Halal products we certify.

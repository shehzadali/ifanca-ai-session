Back to All Herb & Veggie-Baked Spicy Mackerel Maryam Funmilayo June 30, 2018 Copied! Servings: 8-10 Ingredients 6 fresh, medium-sized mackerel fish 2 medium-sized tomatoes 2 medium-sized red and green bell peppers 1 tablespoon of turmeric 1 tablespoon of cumin 1 tablespoon of paprika 1 tablespoon of garlic powder 1 tablespoon of black powder 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley ½ piece of jalapeno ½ piece of habanero 2 medium-sized lemon fruits 2 tablespoons of olive oil Step 1 Preheat oven to 350°F. Step 2 Spread the olive oil on the baking pan evenly. Step 3 Rinse each mackerel fish very well by de-veining all of them properly as well as cutting off their fins and gills. Step 4 Season them with all the spices above except for the peppers, tomatoes, and herbs (parsley and cilantro). These are chopped separately. Step 5 Lastly, marinate the mackerel with lemon juice and keep in the refrigerator for 20 minutes. Step 6 After 20 minutes, spread the mackerel in the baking pan, add the chopped tomatoes, peppers, parsley, and cilantro on top of the mackerel, and place in the preheated oven. Step 7 Bake at 350°F for 60 minutes. Step 8 Serve with beans, pasta, rice, fried plantain, grated, soaked cassava flour (known as 'garri') or simply eat alone.

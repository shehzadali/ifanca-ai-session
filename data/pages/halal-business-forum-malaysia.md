Coreventus Malaysia is organizing the Halal Business Forum to be held on the 15th and 16th of August 2006 at the Ritz-Carlton, Kuala Lumpur. The Halal Business Forum will give you insights from a business perspective ranging from fundamental principles, government initiatives, case studies and logistics and Islamic financing. The Halal Business Forum will feature speakers from the food industry, banking and Islamic organizations and will cover: Employing Principles and Fundamentals of Halal Business Evaluating Halal Quality Standards Marketing Halal Goods Internationally Supporting Halal Business through Halal Financing For more information on the Halal Business Forum, go to www.coreventus.com/HalalForum.htm .

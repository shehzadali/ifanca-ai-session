Back to All Savory Morning Porridge Jin Ohneiser December 31, 2017 Copied! Servings: 2-4 Ingredients 1 cup whole grains (brown rice/red rice/purple rice/sorghum/quinoa or a combination of all) 10 cups water (or halal chicken or veggie broth; preferably homemade) 2 tablespoons toasted sesame oil ½ cup scallions, chopped ¼ cup ginger, minced ½ cup goji berries (optional) salt and pepper to taste Step 1 At night, place grains and water (or broth) into a crock pot. Step 2 Cook overnight on the low setting. Step 3 In the morning, serve porridge into bowls. Step 4 Sprinkle each bowl of porridge with toasted sesame oil, scallions, ginger, salt and pepper, and goji berries (optional), and serve.

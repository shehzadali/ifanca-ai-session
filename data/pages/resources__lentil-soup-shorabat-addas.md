Back to All Lentil Soup “Shorabat Addas” Alia Shalabi March 31, 2018 Copied! Ingredients 2 cups red lentils 9-10 cups water 1 teaspoon cumin ½ teaspoon turmeric 1 tablespoon salt ½ teaspoon pepper 1 large onion, diced 2 tablespoons olive oil juice of one lemon Step 1 Rinse lentils well. Add to a large pot with the water. Bring to a boil, then cover pot and reduce heat slightly. Cook for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, or until lentils are tender. Step 2 While the lentils are cooking, heat a sauté pan over medium heat. Once hot, add olive oil. Sauté the onions until golden brown. Step 3 When the lentils are tender, add cumin, turmeric, salt, pepper, and sautéed onions. Step 4 Simmer for 5 to 10 minutes. Turn off heat and stir in lemon juice. Step 5 Serve with lemon wedges. Enjoy!

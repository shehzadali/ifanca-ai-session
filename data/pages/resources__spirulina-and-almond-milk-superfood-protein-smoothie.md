Back to All Spirulina and Almond Milk Superfood Protein Smoothie YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad MS, RD, LD December 31, 2019 Copied! Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 cup almond milk 1 tablespoon almond butter 1 tablespoon Spirulina powder ¼ teaspoon vanilla powder 1 cup baby spinach 1 banana, frozen 1 cup ice (optional) Step 1 Combine ingredients in blender. Step 2 Blend until smooth and creamy. Step 3 Serve chilled.

Back to All Veggie and Egg Scramble Meriam Jabali September 30, 2018 Copied! Servings: 2 Ingredients 2-4 tablespoons of olive oil 1 small onion, diced 2-4 cloves garlic, chopped or minced (optional to taste) 1 large tomato, diced 1 head broccoli, chopped small 4 large eggs, lightly beaten ¼ teaspoon salt ½ teaspoon black pepper 2 tablespoons parsley, roughly chopped Step 1 In large skillet, put 2 tablespoons olive oil, heat a couple of minutes on medium heat, then add onions and garlic. Sauté about 5 minutes till tender. Step 2 Add tomatoes and broccoli. Continue sautéing on medium for an additional 10-15 minutes. Step 3 Add lightly beaten eggs, salt, and pepper and as they cook gently fold them in with veggies till they are fully cooked. Step 4 Divide delicious veggie eggs into 2 plates, garnish each plate with parsley. Serve with sliced avocado and or 2 slices of melon.

The Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America announces the 7th International Halal Food Conference in Durban, South Africa Halal Food 2005 Theme: A Window to a Halal World The Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America (IFANCA) is co-sponsoring a conference on halal food in conjunction with the 1st International Halal Food Conference by the South African National Halaal Authority (SANHA). *Venue of Conference: Hilton Durban 12/14 Walnut Road Durban, South Africa 4001 *Dates: Thursday, September 8, 2005 – Friday, September 9, 2005 *Conference Fees: US$295.00 This 7th International Halal Food Conference will feature guest speakers from several Muslim countries and Europe who will discuss important issues like import regulations, halal certification programs and how they benefit domestic and international markets in a global environment. The conference is tailored for food industry professionals, academicians, students, government agencies and exporters. Lectures and discussions to be held during this conference will be beneficial and rewarding for all attendees. You and your colleagues are invited to participate in this conference.

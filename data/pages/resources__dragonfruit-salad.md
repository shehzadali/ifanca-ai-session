Back to All Dragonfruit Salad YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD September 30, 2014 Copied! Servings: 2-3 Ingredients 2 dragonfruit, chilled 1 cup chopped mixed fresh fruit 2-3 lettuce leaves (optional) 1 tablespoon fresh mint leaves, thinly sliced (optional) 1 tablespoon honey Step 1 Cut dragonfruit in half lengthwise and scoop out the white fruit center. Step 2 Cut the white fruit in chunks. Step 3 Keep the red dragon fruit peel as a serving bowl. Step 4 Add fruit in a medium bowl with other fresh mixed fruit and mint leaves. Step 5 Lightly drizzle honey over fruit and gently toss it all together. Step 6 Line dragon fruit peel with lettuce and spoon fruit into the peel for serving.

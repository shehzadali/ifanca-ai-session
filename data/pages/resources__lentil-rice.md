Ingredients 1 cup dry lentils, rinsed 2 teaspoon salt 1 tablespoon ground cumin 1 tablespoon garlic powder ¾ cup white basmati rice, rinsed 2 tablespoons olive oil ¼ cup vegetable oil 3 white onions, sliced water as needed Step 1 Soak the lentils in 2½ cups water. In a sauce pan, add salt, cumin, and garlic powder and simmer over low heat until softened. Step 2 Add rice and olive oil. Cover the pot and continue cooking until the lentils and rice are tender. Step 3 Heat the cooking oil in a skillet over medium heat, add onions and fry until brown. Add the onions to the rice and lentil mixture. Serve hot.

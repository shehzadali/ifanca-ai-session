Back to All Oat Crisps with Cranberries and Orange Malika Ameen September 30, 2012 Copied! These elegant cookies require only a few minutes to put together. They have the toasty and warm flavor of brown sugar and molasses and a brightness from orange and cranberries. They are a quick and easy holiday treat to share with loved ones. Ingredients 10 tablespoons Organic Valley® unsalted butter 1 cup firmly packed dark brown sugar ½ cup granulated sugar 1 vanilla bean, seeds scraped (optional) ½ cup dark corn syrup 1 tablespoon grated orange zest 1 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg ¼ teaspoon salt 3 cups thick rolled oats 1 cup sliced Wonderful® almonds with skin 1 cup dried cranberries, roughly chopped Step 1 Preheat oven to 350°F degrees. Step 2 In a microwave safe medium sized bowl, combine sugars, vanilla bean and orange zest. Gently rub together with fingers until sugar looks slightly coarse. Step 3 Add butter and corn syrup. Microwave for 1-2 minutes until butter is melted. Whisk until smooth. Step 4 Add in nutmeg, oats, almonds and cranberries. Mix until well incorporated. Step 5 Line a baking tray with parchment paper. Step 6 Place one tablespoon of oat mixture on baking tray, leaving several inches between each. Step 7 Press cookies gently with the palm of your hand to help it spread evenly when baking. Step 8 Bake crisps for 12-13 minutes until golden brown and bubbly. Cool for several minutes and then remove from baking tray, Step 9 Cool and serve or store in an airtight container up to 4 days.

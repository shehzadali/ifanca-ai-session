Back to All Homemade Hot Cocoa Joan Gray December 31, 2013 Copied! Prep Time: 5 minutes Cook Time: 10 minutes Servings: 4-5 Ingredients ¼ cup cocoa powder ½ cup sugar water, enough to dissolve sugar and powder pinch salt 4 cups milk Step 1 In a heavy saucepan, mix cocoa powder, sugar, salt, and add water to dissolve. Step 2 Stir until it turns into syrup. Step 3 Add milk and heat to drinking temperature but not to a boil. Step 4 Pour into cups and serve. Optional Add halal marshmallows or whipping cream on top.

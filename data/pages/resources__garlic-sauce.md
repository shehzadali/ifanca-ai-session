Back to All Garlic Sauce Masooda Rahman March 31, 2006 Copied! Ingredients ½ cup sour cream ½ cup mayonnaise 3-4 cloves garlic minced 1 tablespoon lemon juice 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce salt and pepper to taste Step 1 Whisk all ingredients together until smooth and serve. This sauce is also great on salads and as a dip for veggies.

Servings: 6 | Time: 1 hour 30 minutes Ingredients 2 lbs halal beef pasinday (beef filet, sliced approximately 1/4 inch thick) 2 cups water 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste 1/2 tablespoon red chili powder (adjust to taste) 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder 1/2 tablespoon salt 3/4 cup oil 1 tablespoon freshly ground black pepper Step 1 Place the beef in a large pot and add the water, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, and salt. Step 2 Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and simmer until the beef is about 90% cooked and most of the water has evaporated. Step 3 Add oil and black pepper to the pot. Continue cooking over medium heat until the remaining liquid has reduced. Step 4 Increase the heat slightly and stir-fry the beef for a few minutes, turning occasionally, until the oil separates and the meat is lightly browned and coated with the spices. Step 5 Serve hot with daal (lentils) and steamed rice for a comforting, traditional meal.

Back to All Sparkling Pomegranate and Mint Cooler Saira Mohiuddin June 30, 2016 Copied! Servings: 2-4 Ingredients 4 cups pomegranate seeds 1 cup water 1 cup POM® Wonderful Pomegranate Juice 4 tablespoons simple syrup 1 bunch mint 1 cup sparkling lemonade Step 1 Blend pomegranate seeds with water; strain mixture into pitcher. Discard remaining seeds. Step 2 Stir in pomegranate juice and simple syrup. Step 3 Thinly slice 10 to 15 mint leaves; add to mixture. Step 4 Stir in sparkling lemonade. Step 5 Serve over ice; garnish with mint leaves, if desired.

Servings: 4 Time: 30 minutes Ingredients ¼ cup water 1 small yellow onion, diced 2 cups white navy beans, cooked and rinsed ⅓ cup crushed tomatoes 1½ tablespoons fancy molasses 1 tablespoon maple syrup 1 tablespoon halal mustard 1 tablespoon lemon juice 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar ½ teaspoon paprika ½ teaspoon sea salt Step 1 Add water to a medium pot and bring to a boil. Step 2 Add the onion and simmer for 5 minutes. Step 3 Stir in the remaining ingredients and simmer for 15 minutes. Serve and enjoy! Notes One serving size is approximately ½ cup of beans. Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 5 days. Top with fresh parsley for extra flavor!

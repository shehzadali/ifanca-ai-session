IFANCA is proud to be a partner and supporter of the Saudi International Halal Expo 2022 . The event, which takes place from November 7 to 9, 2022 , intends to bring together people and businesses from all over the world to discuss trends in the halal industry. The expo will be held at the Riyadh International Convention & Exhibition Center in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia , where both exhibitors and visitors from various sectors can network with one another. There will also be a joint conference featuring lectures, training, and more from halal professionals. For more information, visit the Saudi International Halal Expo 2022 website at www.saudihalalexpo.com . From there, you can book an exhibition booth, register as a visitor or hosted buyer, or sign up to attend the conference.

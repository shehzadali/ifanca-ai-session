Back to All Chickpea Soup Chef Demetrios Haralambatos September 30, 2020 Copied! Ingredients 3 large carrots, chopped 2 celery stalks, chopped 2 large cans chickpeas ¾ cups olive oil 3 large onions, sliced thinly 1 can (8 ounces) tomato sauce 1 can (16 ounces) whole tomatoes, loosely crushed salt and pepper to taste Step 1 Place all ingredients in a pot and bring to a boil. Step 2 Simmer until carrots are cooked, occasionally adding water if needed. Step 3 Season to taste. Note If using dried chickpeas, soak them overnight and drain twice before proceeding with the steps above.

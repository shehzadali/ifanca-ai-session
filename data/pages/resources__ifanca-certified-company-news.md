The Noni tree is native to Tahiti and is around 2000 years old. All its parts are useful and beneficial for various health reasons. The traditional Polynesians pick the noni before it is fully ripe and place it in a jar in direct sunlight. When fully ripe, the noni is mashed into a puree and the juice is extracted through a cloth. For people who are not used to the fruit, it has a slightly objectionable taste, that the processors were able to overcome using only natural fruit juices. It is necessary to harvest Morinda fruit at the correct stage of maturity in order to maintain the quality of the juice.

Under the auspices of Minister of Justice and of Awqaf and Islamic Affairs in the State of Kuwait H.E. Shereedah Abdullah Al-Moasherji The Second Gulf Conference on Halal Industry and its Services Entitled “Towards the Effective Management of the Halal Industry” 10-12 Rabi’ Al-Awal 1434 Hijri 22 – 24 January 2013 Crown Plaza Hotel*-Farwaniyah, State of Kuwait Organizers Ministry of Awqaf and Islamic Affairs Kuwait Institute for Scientific Research In collaboration with: PAI, GSO, SFDA, WAMY, LPPOM MUI, IIHA, UPM, UMS, UQ, HMC, IFANCA, ASIDCOM, GIMDES, HFFIA, FIANZ, IHIA, WHC, SANHA and HC *For hotel reservations at the Crown Plaza and visa for staying at the Crown Plaza must be through: Mrs. Itedal Al-Bakri email: etidal.bakri@Ihg.com ; mobile: 0096596961416 For booth reservation must be through: Mr. Ahmed Al-Barbary email: info@empire-cs.com ; mobile: 0096597298910 For any further information please contact: Dr. Hani Mansour Al-Mazeedi email: mazeedi@hotmail.com ; mobile 0096597498500 Objectives: To gather specialized human and technical resources to study the current status of the control of Halal. To suggest practical solutions to overcome the obstacles facing the Halal industry. To assess the wrong accreditation practices of Halal certificates. To set a future concept for a Halal model that suits Islamic countries. To increase awareness and necessary training to the Halal products and services.

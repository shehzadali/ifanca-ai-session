Back to All Sprouted Lentil Salad Maryam Funmilayo September 30, 2020 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 2 cups green sprouted lentils ½ small red onion 2 small Roma tomatoes 1 teaspoon black seeds 1 teaspoon organic chia seeds 1 teaspoon organic flax seeds 1 teaspoon organic shelled hemp seeds 1 teaspoon ground black pepper ½ cup lemon juice Step 1 In a colander, rinse the green sprouted lentils with cold water. Drain and set aside. Step 2 Rinse, peel, and dice the onions. Step 3 Rinse thoroughly and chop the tomatoes. Step 4 Combine the lentils, onions, and tomatoes and toss with remaining ingredients. Step 5 Mix all together. Step 6 Garnish with lemon juice. Step 7 Mix again. Step 8 Serve immediately and enjoy.

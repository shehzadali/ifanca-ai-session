Back to All Knock-Out Ramadan Punch Haleema Siddiqui June 30, 2009 Copied! Prep Time: 10 minutes Total Time: 10 minutes Servings: 2 large punchbowls Ingredients 1 2L bottle lemon-lime soda 1 2L bottle orange soda 1 2L bottle cranberry gingerale 1 2L bottle lemonade 1 2L bottle Hawaiian Punch 1.5 qt. rainbow sherbet ice cream 6-8 thin, round lemon slices Step 1 Pour ½ of each drink in large punch bowl and stir. Step 2 Slowly add large scoops of ice cream until it foams, but do not let it overflow. Step 3 Throw in sliced lemon to garnish. Step 4 Pour and serve.

Back to All Cabbage, Apple, and Kohlrabi Slaw YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD June 30, 2014 Copied! Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 large kohlrabi juice of ½ a lemon ¼ cup dill, chopped ½ clove garlic, minced 1 teaspoon mayo or halal Greek yogurt salt and pepper to taste 3 tablespoons olive oil ½ head Savoy cabbage, shredded 1 large Granny Smith apple, cored and thinly sliced Step 1 Peel kohlrabi and cut into thin slices, like matchsticks. Step 2 Whisk together lemon juice, dill, garlic, mayo or Greek yogurt, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Step 3 Whisk in the olive oil and toss with the kohlrabi, cabbage, and apple. Step 4 Add salt and pepper to taste.

Back to All Roast Breast of Lamb Moroccan Style Hanane El Rhalib, Graphic Designer March 31, 2013 Copied! Ingredients 3 pounds of lamb breast — halal certified 1 onion ½ cup of vegetable oil 1 tablespoon of smen (ghee) — clarified butter 3 garlic cloves 1 teaspoon of salt (to taste) ½ teaspoon of turmeric powder 2 teaspoons of paprika 1 teaspoon of ginger powder a large pinch of saffron Step 1 Dice the onion and mince the garlic. Step 2 Mix the spices in ½ cup of water. Step 3 Make few slits in the meat. Cover the meat with the spice mix and the garlic. Step 4 In a cooking pan, cook the meat with the vegetable oil, clarified butter, and the onion. Add 2 cups of water to the meat. Step 5 Cover the pan and let the meat cook on high heat until fully cooked (that will take approximately 1 hour). Rotate the meat from time to time in the pan so it is cooked from all sides. Step 6 Once the meat is cooked, place it in a baking pan and pour few tablespoons of the sauce on top. Step 7 Broil the meat in the oven for 5 minutes. Step 8 Pour the rest of the sauce on the meat and serve immediately.

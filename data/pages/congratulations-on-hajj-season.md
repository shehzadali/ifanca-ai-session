With the arrival of the Hajj season, IFANCA wishes everyone Eid Mubarak. May the blessed season bring you a fresh start, inner peace and spiritual fulfillment. For those fortunate enough to be blessed with the opportunity to perform Hajj, we ask ALLAH, Subhanahu wa ta’ala, to accept your efforts and your sacrifices reward you with Paradise. Thul Hijjah is the 12th month in the Islamic calendar, known as the Hijra calendar. This is one of the four sacred months, and is the month of the Hajj, or Pilgrimage to Makkah, as it is known in the West. Hajj is one of the five pillars of Islam and it incorporates the blessed holiday of Eid-ul-Adha.

Back to All Roasted Brussels Sprouts Tayyaba Syed June 30, 2013 Copied! Serving Size: 3 | Total Prep Time: 45-50 minutes Ingredients 1½ pounds brussels sprouts 3 tablespoons olive oil ¾ teaspoon salt ½ teaspoon ground black pepper Step 1 Preheat oven to 400 degrees F. Step 2 Cut off brown ends of the sprouts and pull off any yellow leaves. Step 3 Mix oil, salt and black pepper in a bowl. Step 4 Toss/coat the sprouts with the spice mix. Step 5 Bake and roast for 35-40 minutes in the oven.

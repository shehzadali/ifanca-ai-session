Back to All Spicy Guacamole Maryam Funmilayo September 30, 2021 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 3 ripe avocados, peeled and pitted ½ cup garlic, finely chopped ½ cup serrano peppers, finely chopped ¼ cup fresh moringa leaves, minced 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice ½ teaspoon black pepper, freshly ground ½ teaspoon ground cumin ½ teaspoon black seeds Step 1 Rinse, peel, and mash the avocados in a medium-sized bowl using a fork Step 2 Add the remaining ingredients and stir well. Step 3 Cover and chill.

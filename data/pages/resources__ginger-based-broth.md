Back to All Ginger-Based Broth Maryam Funmilayo March 31, 2022 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 3 cups water 7 large organic roma tomatoes 3 medium organic red bell peppers 3 small organic habanero peppers 3 medium organic onions 1 cup unpeeled organic ginger, grated (can be old and/or dehydrated) Step 1 Boil the water in a large pot. Step 2 Add the tomatoes, peppers, onions, and ginger to the water. Step 3 Cook for one hour. Step 4 After one hour, remove from heat and let the broth cool completely. Step 5 Blend the broth and use it for meat, fish, or chicken stew.

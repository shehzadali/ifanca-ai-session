Back to All Chicken Soup & Sandwich Aisha Kureishy September 30, 2013 Copied! Preparation Time: 5 minutes | Cooking Time: 20-25 minutes Ingredients 2-4 medium lean chicken breast pieces 1 medium onion 2 whole garlic salt to taste 8-12 whole black peppers 1 green banana pepper (optional) 5 cups of water bread tomato, lettuce & cucumber for garnish Soup Directions Wash and remove extra fat from the chicken. Add to the pot with peeled onion, garlic and 5 cups of water. Add salt, whole black peppers and banana pepper. Cook for 20-25 minutes. Remove chicken pieces from the pot, let those cool down. Strain the chicken broth into soup bowls. Take one chicken breast piece, shred it and add it to your broth. Garnish it with fresh coriander leaves and enjoy. Sandwich Directions Shred rest of the chicken pieces; add mayonnaise, and mash it thoroughly. Spread it on your favorite bread and garnish.

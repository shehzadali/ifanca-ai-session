Back to All Slow Cooker Cashew Ginger Chicken YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD June 30, 2014 Copied! Servings: 4-6 Ingredients 2 pounds halal boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cubed ¼ cup flour ½ teaspoon black pepper 1 tablespoon olive oil ¼ cup soy sauce 2 tablespoons lemon juice 2 tablespoons ketchup 1 tablespoon brown sugar 1 clove garlic, minced ½ teaspoon fresh ginger, grated ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes ½ cup cashews Step 1 Combine black pepper and flour in resealable storage bag. Add chicken and shake to coat. Step 2 Heat oil in skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and brown, about 2 minutes on each side. Place browned chicken in slow cooker. Step 3 Combine soy sauce, lemon juice, ketchup, sugar, garlic, ginger, and pepper flakes in small bowl; pour over chicken. Step 4 Cook on low for 3 to 4 hours. Add cashews and stir. Serve over rice.

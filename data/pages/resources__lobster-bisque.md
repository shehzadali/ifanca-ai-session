Back to All Lobster Bisque Submitted by Customculinary.com for Food Service Professionals September 30, 2006 Copied! Ingredients 6 ounces butter or margarine 6 ounces all-purpose flour 3 quarts water 5 ounces Custom Culinary lobster base dash of red pepper, ground 1 quart cream/half and half optional: 8 ounces lobster meat, cooked, small diced (To add as a garnish—½ ounce cooked lobster meat per portion) Step 1 In a 2-gallon sauce pot over medium-high heat, melt butter and blend in flour. Cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring constantly. Step 2 Add water, lobster base, and red pepper. Blend thoroughly to eliminate lumps. Heat to a boil, stirring constantly. Reduce heat and simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Step 3 Add cream and cook 2-3 minutes, then remove from heat. Makes about 1 gallon.

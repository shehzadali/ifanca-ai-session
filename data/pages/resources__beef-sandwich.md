Back to All Beef Sandwich Salma Siddiqui September 30, 2012 Copied! Feeds one hungry football team Ingredients 5 lbs. chunk of lean beef, washed 1 cup yogurt, whipped 1 tablespoon black pepper powder 1 teaspoon ginger and garlic paste 1 tablespoon salt For Sandwich whole wheat bread slices mayonnaise to taste Instructions Mix together ginger garlic paste, black pepper powder, salt and yogurt to make marinade. Add beef and mix well. Marinate for 30 minutes. Cook on low heat for 3 hours, turning beef chunk regularly. When the water dries and the meat is cooked, let it cool, then slice thinly. Spread mayonnaise on meat slices and stack slices, in whole wheat bread to make a sandwich.

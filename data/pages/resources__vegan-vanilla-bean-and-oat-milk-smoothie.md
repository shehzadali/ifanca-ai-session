Back to All Vegan Vanilla Bean and Oat Milk Smoothie YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad MS, RD, LD December 31, 2019 Copied! Servings: 4–6 Ingredients 1 cup oat milk (vegan milk) 1 cup spinach, fresh 1 medium banana, frozen ½ cup strawberries, frozen ¼ cup blueberries, frozen ¼ teaspoon vanilla bean powder 1 Natural Delight Medjool date for extra sweetness 1 cup of ice Step 1 Combine ingredients in blender. Step 2 Blend until smooth and creamy. Step 3 Serve chilled.

We are proud to announce that IFANCA has received Halal Accreditation Agency (HAK) accreditation! To celebrate this important achievement, IFANCA President and CEO Dr. Chaudry was invited to the HAK office in Ankara on May 29, 2023, to accept IFANCA’s accreditation certificate . According to Turkish law, HAK accreditation is obligatory for organizations issuing halal conformity certificates for products and services for import to Türkiye (Turkey) from around the world wherever IFANCA conducts audits and issues halal certifications. This amendment officially enters into force on June 4, 2023, and is valid until 2028. HAK accreditation is based on OIC/SMIIC 2 standards. All clients wishing to export to Türkiye must follow SMIIC standards and will be audited by IFANCA according to those standards. IFANCA will continue to comply with SMIIC 2 standard and HAK requirements to ensure that it maintains this accreditation. For questions about HAK accreditation and what this means for your business, please get in touch by emailing halal@ifanca.org .

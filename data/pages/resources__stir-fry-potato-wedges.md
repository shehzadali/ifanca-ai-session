Back to All Stir Fry Potato Wedges From the Kitchen of Aisha Kureishy September 30, 2010 Copied! Servings: 6-8 Prep Time: 10 minutes Cooking Time: 30 minutes Ingredients 4-6 medium potatoes (Approximately 2 pounds) 1 teaspoon fresh chives (optional) ½ teaspoon halal steak spice mix salt and black pepper to taste Step 1 Cut potatoes lengthwise into ten or twelve wedges. Step 2 Sprinkle salt, black pepper and steak spice mix. Mix well. Step 3 Sprinkle some water over potatoes and cover with a microwave safe plate. Make sure potato wedges do not overlap. Microwave for three minutes. Step 4 With kitchen gloves, flip the plates gently and nicely without dropping the wedges. You may use more water to keep potatoes soft and moist. Step 5 Microwave the wedges for another three minutes. Step 6 In a large skillet, warm oil on medium heat. Step 7 Add all the potato wedges to the skillet without splashing the hot oil. Step 8 Stir fry until golden brown on each side. Step 9 Cover the pan and leave the potatoes to sauté on low heat for five to ten minutes.

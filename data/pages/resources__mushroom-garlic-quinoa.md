Servings: 4 Time: 20 minutes Ingredients 1 cup quinoa, uncooked 1¾ cups water 1½ teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil 2 cups mushrooms, thinly sliced 2 garlic cloves, minced sea salt and black pepper to taste Step 1 Combine the quinoa and water in a pot. Place over high heat and bring to a boil. Step 2 Once the water is boiling, reduce to a simmer and cover. Let it simmer for 12 to 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Step 3 Remove lid, fluff with a fork, and set aside. Step 4 While the quinoa cooks, heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Sauté sliced mushrooms until soft. Step 5 Add garlic and sauté for another 1 to 2 minutes. Step 6 In a bowl, combine mushrooms with quinoa and season with salt and pepper. Enjoy! Notes: Serve it with roast beef, honey garlic chicken thighs, or soup. Refrigerate leftovers in an air-tight container for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 1 month. If freezing, make sure to squeeze out all the air and flatten your freezer bag to reduce the chances of freezer burn and optimize storage space.

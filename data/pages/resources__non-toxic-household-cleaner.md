Back to All Non-Toxic Household Cleaner Erika Elmuts December 31, 2016 Copied! Ingredients 16-ounce glass spray bottle 30 drops Sentry ¼ cup rubbing alcohol 1¾ cups water Step 1 Place oil and alcohol into the spray bottle. Step 2 Fill the rest of the bottle with water. Step 3 Use to clean countertops, bathrooms, floors, etc.

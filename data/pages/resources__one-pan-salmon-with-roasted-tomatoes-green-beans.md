Back to All One-Pan Salmon with Roasted Tomatoes & Green Beans Omolara Funmilayo December 31, 2022 Copied! Servings: 4 Time: 25 minutes Ingredients 4 cups green beans, washed and trimmed 2 cups cherry tomatoes 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil 1¼-pound salmon filet sea salt & black pepper to taste Step 1 Preheat oven to 500°F. Step 2 Place green beans and cherry tomatoes in a mixing bowl and toss with olive oil. Season with sea salt and black pepper. Step 3 Transfer to a baking sheet and bake in the oven for 10 minutes. Step 4 Season your salmon filets with sea salt and black pepper. Step 5 Remove veggies from the oven and place salmon filets on top. Place back in the oven and bake for another 7 to 10 minutes, or until the salmon flakes off with a fork. Step 6 Divide veggies between plates and top with salmon. Enjoy.

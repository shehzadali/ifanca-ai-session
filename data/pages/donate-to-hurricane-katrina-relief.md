Following the tsunami disaster in Asia, IFANCA set up the IFANCA Relief Fund to help the victims of that disaster. With the disastrous effects of Hurricane Katrina, IFANCA is soliciting donations for the victims of the hurricane. 100% of all contributions received will be sent to the relief effort. Please send your contributions to: IFANCA Relief Fund – Hurricane Katrina 5901 N. Cicero Avenue, Suite 309 Chicago, IL 60646 USA Tel: +001-773-283-3708 Fax: +001-773-283-3973 Or contact IFANCA for further information. Thank you for your support.

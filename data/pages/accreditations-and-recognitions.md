Accreditations and Recognitions We’re Globally Accepted Our accredited certification process is recognized and trusted by the world’s Muslim majority countries Selecting the right certifying body can make or break your business. When researching halal certifying bodies, it is important to ensure that they are recognized and accredited by the proper authorities. Accreditation bodies and regulatory bodies help create transparency and trust for halal consumers  to select appropriate products that meet their needs. Regulatory bodies exist throughout the world to serve their respective countries, and establish standards and halal requirements. IFANCA strictly adheres to these standards and requirements to certify companies’ products as halal, empowering them to expand and grow in the global halal market.

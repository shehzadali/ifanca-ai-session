Back to All Banana Bread Alia Shalabi December 31, 2016 Copied! Servings: 10-12 Ingredients 3 ripe bananas ½ cup sugar 1 egg 1½ cups flour of your choice cup ¼ vegetable oil 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon baking soda ½ cup chopped walnuts, optional Step 1 Preheat oven to 325°F. Step 2 Mash bananas with a fork. Step 3 Stir in remaining ingredients until well combined. Step 4 Pour into a greased loaf pan and bake for 50 to 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Step 5 Cool for 5 minutes; remove from pan and cool completely on a wire rack.

The culinary world has seen countless trends, but the rise of “swicy” food is captivating food lovers worldwide. “Swicy,” a fusion of sweet and spicy flavors, appeals to adventurous eaters who enjoy a complexity of flavors. But how did this trend emerge in the USA, and why is it becoming so popular? What is Swicy? “Swicy, a blend of “sweet” and “spicy,” describes foods that balance these two seemingly opposite flavors.” Though rooted in various global cuisines, swicy has evolved into a unique modern trend. As Sebnem Karasu, CEO of Karasu Consulting and panelist at an August 2024 Chicagoland Food Science Foundation event, explains, “The 2024 flavor trend, especially among Gen Z, is swicy—sweet and spicy.” This blend creates a dynamic flavor profile that makes spiciness more accessible by tempering it with sweetness, broadening its appeal to those who might typically avoid hot and spicy foods. The Emerging of Swicy Trend in the USA Though it’s unclear who coined the term “swicy,” the flavor profile has long existed in traditional cuisines from South America, Korea, and South Asia. An example is Korean gochujang sauce, known for its balance of sweet and spicy. The trend has been growing over the past decade, influenced by evolving consumer tastes, wellness awareness, and culinary innovations. As people in the USA continue to explore world foods, they also seek ways to incorporate wellness into their favorite dishes, often by adding spices. Cultural Influences on Swicy Foods Many global cuisines have long embraced the fusion of sweet and spicy flavors, significantly impacting the swicy trend in the U.S. Mexican cuisine, which uses chili with chocolate or fruit, and Thai dishes, pairing spicy chilies with sweet coconut milk or tamarind, are notable influences. The popularity of Korean cuisine in the U.S. has also boosted swicy’s appeal. Korean fried chicken, often glazed in a sweet and spicy sauce, has become a favorite. This global influence has permeated American dining, bringing swicy flavors into foods like hot dogs, which are now often topped with jalapeños, Sriracha, or honey chipotle. The swicy wave extends to chicken wings, burgers, and even desserts, where chocolate bars and ice creams incorporate spices like chili or cayenne, creating a unique twist on classics. The Role of Social Media in the Swicy Trend No conversation about modern food trends is complete without mentioning social media. Platforms like Instagram and TikTok have been instrumental in popularizing swicy food among Gen Z. Food influencers and chefs have turned to these platforms to showcase creative recipes, often featuring swicy combinations. Viral challenges like “spicy food mukbangs” and taste tests of sweet-and-spicy snacks have further pushed this trend into the limelight. “Gen Z craves novelty, and swicy food aligns perfectly with their desire for bold flavors. Social media provides them with a window into culinary innovation, allowing them to discover and try new swicy combinations.” Scientific Perspective on Swicy Trend Combining sweetness with spiciness enhances the sensory experience, making it enjoyable for a broader audience. Research supports the appeal of spicy foods and their health benefits. According to Jiang (2019), spices like black pepper have compounds that positively impact mood and cognitive function, partly explaining their appeal. For example, Ao et al. (2022) finds that consuming chili peppers and spicy foods may reduce the risk of mortality from cardiovascular and cerebrovascular issues. With growing exposure to global cuisines, swicy dishes are likely to continue evolving and gaining popularity.

Servings: 7 Ingredients 7 halal chicken thighs juice of ½ lemon 2 tablespoons olive oil 3 tablespoons harissa paste ¼ teaspoon white ground pepper ¼ teaspoon garlic powder ¼ teaspoon cumin ¼ teaspoon chili powder ¼ teaspoon paprika ¼ teaspoon onion powder 1½ teaspoon pink Himalayan salt Step 1 Preheat your oven to 350°F. Step 2 Place the chicken thighs in a bowl. Squeeze in lemon juice and drain. Step 3 Mix in the harissa paste, spices, and olive oil. Let it marinate for 10 minutes. Step 4 Make horizontal cuts across the chicken thigh. These cuts should be deep enough to touch bone. Step 5 Heat a grill pan until it is really hot. Pack in the chicken thighs and sear both sides for 5 minutes. Start with the chicken skin side of the thigh for crispy skin. Step 6 Transfer the seared chicken to a baking dish. Step 7 Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes. Step 8 Serve with mashed potatoes or a vegetable side dish.

Servings: 2 Time: 25 minutes Ingredients 2 cups water 1-inch piece of ginger, peeled 4-5 cloves 5-6 peppercorns 5-6 fresh basil leaves 1/2 teaspoon honey 2-inch piece of cinnamon stick Optional: Mulethi (licorice root) Step 1 In a deep saucepan, bring the water to a boil. Step 2 While the water heats, crush the ginger, cloves, black peppercorns, and cinnamon stick using a mortar and pestle. Step 3 Once the water is boiling, add the crushed ingredients along with the basil leaves to the saucepan. Step 4 Lower the heat to medium and let the mixture simmer for about 20 minutes, reducing the liquid to half. Step 5 Strain the liquid into a glass or mug and stir in honey. Serve warm. Notes: Kadha is a natural remedy for coughs, colds, and seasonal flu, thanks to its antiviral and anti-inflammatory properties.
The basil leaves help reduce mucus, while the spices soothe the throat and boost immunity.
Regular consumption of Kadha may also help in preventing kidney stones and improving overall kidney health.

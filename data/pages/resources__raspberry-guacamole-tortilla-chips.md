Back to All Raspberry Guacamole & Tortilla Chips Aisha Kureishy June 30, 2009 Copied! Prep Time: 5 minutes Total Time: 5 minutes Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 cold avocado 1½ cups cold raspberries 1 tsp. honey ¼ tsp. salt (optional) tortilla chips Step 1 Slice avocado in half & spoon out insides and press into mixing bowl until smooth. Step 2 Add clean raspberries and mix until mashed. Step 3 Add honey and/or salt if desired. Step 4 Serve immediately with tortilla chips or use as a filling for tacos.

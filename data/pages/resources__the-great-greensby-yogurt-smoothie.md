Back to All The Great Greensby, Yogurt Smoothie YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD June 30, 2017 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 1 cup frozen peaches 1 cup frozen pineapples 1 cup fresh spinach 1 cup baby kale 1 cup IFANCA-certified Greek yogurt 1 ripe banana 1 teaspoon cinnamon Step 1 Combine ingredients in a blender. Step 2 Blend until smooth and creamy. Step 3 Serve with cinnamon sprinkled on top.

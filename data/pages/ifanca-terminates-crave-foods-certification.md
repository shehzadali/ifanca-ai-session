It is with regret that we announce that Crave Foods Inc., Cerritos California, does not produce products that meet IFANCA standards resulting in the termination of halal certification of Crave products. We have asked Crave to immediately stop the use of IFANCA’s registered Crescent M trademark or any reference to their products being certified by IFANCA, either on the product labels or promotional materials. Any use of the IFANCA name or logo is an unauthorized use and in violation of U.S. Trademark Regulations. Also, we urge consumers to report any Crave items which bear the IFANCA logo to us.

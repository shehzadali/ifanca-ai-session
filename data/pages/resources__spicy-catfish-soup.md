Back to All Spicy Catfish Soup Maryam Funmilayo December 31, 2019 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 30 medium-sized Roma tomatoes 3 big white onions ½ cup of dried chile de arbol (stemless) peppers 3 small habanero peppers 1 garlic bulb 3 cups water 2 cups of extra virgin olive oil 2 medium-sized catfish 2 small lemons 1 tablespoon paprika 1 tablespoon thyme 1 tablespoon bay leaves 1 tablespoon ground basil 1 tablespoon ground rosemary 1 tablespoon curry powder 1 tablespoon ground turmeric 1 tablespoon cayenne pepper 1 teaspoon of salt Step 1 Blend the tomatoes, onions, peppers, and garlic together. Step 2 Pour the mixture along with water in a large pot and set aside. Step 3 Cut the catfish into smaller pieces and clean them thoroughly with water. Step 4 Marinade the fish with lemon juice, paprika, thyme, basil, rosemary, curry powder, and turmeric. Step 5 Set aside in a large bowl. Step 6 Add olive oil, cayenne pepper, and salt into the pot. Step 7 Stir very well and boil on very low heat for only 15 minutes. Step 8 Add the catfish into the pot and boil for another 15 minutes. Step 9 Serve alone or with steamed green vegetables.

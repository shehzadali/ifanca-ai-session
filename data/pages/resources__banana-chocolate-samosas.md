Back to All Banana Chocolate Samosas Masooda Rahman March 31, 2006 Copied! Ingredients wonton wrappers sliced bananas—use as many as needed 1 package semi-sweet chocolate chips canola oil for frying Step 1 Take wonton wrapper. On one half, place one piece of banana and 3-4 chocolate chips. Apply water to all edges and seal. Step 2 Heat oil about 3-4 inches deep. Fry just until both sides are golden brown. Step 3 Drain on a paper towel. Serve.

IFANCA is proud to support students who intend to work in the food industry by providing scholarship funds through the Chicagoland Food Science Foundation (CFSF). A total of six IFANCA scholarships are available, totaling $5,000. One freshman applicant will receive a scholarship of $2,500, and five juniors at the Chicago High School for Agricultural Sciences will receive $500 each to cover college application fees and visits. These scholarships will be awarded by CFSF, an organization that seeks to grow and support the next generation of foodservice professionals. Interested students can apply for these and other scholarships on the CFSF website . Applications are due by March 10, 2023. For more information, please see the full announcement here .

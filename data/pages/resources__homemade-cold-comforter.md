Back to All Homemade Cold Comforter Husna T. Ghani December 31, 2013 Copied! Ingredients 1 cup hot green tea 1 tiny piece fresh ginger (about ⅛ to ¼-inch) 1-2 teaspoons pure honey 2-3 cloves 1 small piece fresh cinnamon (about ⅛-inch) 1-2 drops fresh lemon juice Step 1 Stir ginger, honey, cloves, cinnamon, and lemon juice into the green tea. Step 2 Cover and let sit for a few minutes. Step 3 Enjoy!

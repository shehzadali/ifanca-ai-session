IFANCA® 22 nd International Halal Food Conference April 13-15, 2025, Chicago/ Rosemont, Illinois, USA The Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America (IFANCA®) will host its 22 nd International Halal Food Conference from April 13 to April 15, 2025 , at the Hyatt Regency O’Hare in Rosemont, Illinois , USA . The conference theme, “Navigating Change Together: Connecting the Global Halal Community,” reflects the shared effort to meet the increasing demand for halal-certified products worldwide. Click here to view the Final Agenda . We look forward to hearing Speakers from: BPJPH Indonesia JAKIM Malaysia MUIS Singapore SFDA Saudi Arabia SMIIC Türkiye and more! Conference Topics will Include: • Halal Ingredients and Certification Requirements • Global Standards for Halal Certification • The Role of Government and International Agreements in Certification • Opportunities in Domestic Halal Markets • Innovations in Halal Certification Conference Dates & Times: Sunday, April 13, 2025 *VIP Session : 5:30pm – 7:00pm (Diamond & Platinum Sponsors only) General Reception : 6:00pm – 7:00pm Banquet : 7:00pm – 10:00pm Monday, April 14, 2025 Technical Sessions: 9:00am – 5:00pm Tuesday, April 15, 2025 Technical Sessions: 9:00am – 5:00pm Registration: Registration Fee: USD 1,200 per person Registration Link: https://ifanca.regfox.com/ifanca-22nd-international-halal-food-conference (IFANCA clients only) – password required Space is limited, so register early to reserve your spot!

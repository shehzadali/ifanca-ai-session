The Food Export Association of the Midwest USA and Food Export USA – Northeast, in association with the Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America (IFANCA) and Islamic Services Of America (ISA), invites you to attend a free seminar on Halal Certification and Worldwide Halal Trends on Monday, April 28, 2008, during the upcoming Global Food & Style Expo in Chicago.

Servings: 6 Ingredients 1 pound whole mushrooms 2 cubes halal beef boullion ½ cup hot water 1 stick Organic Valley® lightly-salted butter 2 tablespoons white flour ½ cup Organic Valley® heavy cream a dash of pepper ½ cup bread crumbs 1 cup Parmesan cheese Step 1 In a pan, sauté mushrooms in ½ stick butter, remove and set aside. Step 2 In a separate bowl, dissolve beef cubes in hot water. Step 3 In the same pan, melt the other ½ stick butter and blend with flour. Step 4 Add butter, cream, pepper, beef broth and bring the sauce to a simmer. Add the mushrooms now. Step 5 Place the mushroom-sauce mixture in a casserole dish. Step 6 Top with cheese and bread crumbs. Step 7 Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes. Step 8 Voila! It’s this easy!

Back to All “Dodo” (a.k.a. “Fried Plantains”) Maryam Funmilayo June 30, 2018 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 4 ripe, bright yellow plantains 1½ cups of organic, cold pressed, virgin, unrefined, non-GMO avocado or coconut oil Step 1 Wash the plantains and use a sharp knife to peel open the plantains. Step 2 One by one, slice each plantain in a crosswise manner, into a medium-sized bowl. Step 3 Heat up avocado oil or coconut oil in a deep fryer for 3 minutes. Step 4 Place 10 to 12 plantain slices at a time, turning them every 2 to 3 minutes until they are lightly brown on both sides. Step 5 Transfer the fried plantains with a sieve onto a paper towel, to absorb the extra oil. Then transfer to a flat dish. Step 6 Serve with cooked beans or lentils, vegetable stew, jollof rice, soaked cassava flour known as "garri", or simply eat alone as a side dish!

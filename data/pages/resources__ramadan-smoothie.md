Back to All Ramadan Smoothie Kifah Muhammad June 30, 2019 Copied! Servings: 1 Ingredients ½ avocado 3 dates 1 tablespoon honey ½ inch piece fresh ginger, peeled 1½ cups milk 5 mint leaves ½ cup ice Step 1 Place ingredients in a blender. Step 2 Blend till smooth. Step 3 Pour into your favorite glass and enjoy!

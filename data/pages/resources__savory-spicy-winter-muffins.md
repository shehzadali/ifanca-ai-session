Back to All Savory Spicy Winter Muffins YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD December 31, 2016 Copied! Servings: 12-14 Ingredients 2¼ cups flour of your choice ⅔ cup brown sugar ½ cup sugar 2 teaspoons cinnamon ⅛ teaspoon nutmeg ⅛ teaspoon allspice ⅛ teaspoon cloves teaspoon ⅛ teaspoon ginger 2½ teaspoons baking powder ¼ teaspoon salt ½ cup Organic Valley® Organic Butter, softened 1 large egg ¾ cup cooked mashed pumpkin ½ cup Mariani Golden Raisins 1 teaspoon orange zest 1 cup Organic Valley® Organic Buttermilk powdered sugar, for tops as garnish Step 1 Heat oven to 400°F. Grease muffin tin with butter. Step 2 Mix flour, sugars, spices, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl. Cut in butter with pastry blender or a food processor until crumbly. Step 3 Beat egg in a medium bowl; stir in pumpkin, raisins, orange zest, and buttermilk. Add to flour mixture until just blended. Step 4 Fill muffin cups ¾ full. Sprinkle tops with sugar. Step 5 Bake for 20 to 25 minutes until lightly browned and a toothpick comes out of the center clean. Step 6 Serve with apple, pecan, or lemon butter.

Following the death of their 23-year-old daughter, Zunaira, in a fatal car accident in January 2004, Schaumburg residents Syed Zaheer Khader and Zehra Zaheer Khader, agreed to help underwrite a scholarship in their daughter’s memory. Zunaira was a second year student at John Marshall Law School, Chicago. The scholarship will be awarded to a student with demonstrated financial need. Preference will be given to a student who is a member of the Muslim Law Student Association. Naheda Zayyad, a third-year student, has been named the first recipient of the Zunaira Zaheer Khader Memorial Scholarship. Following her graduation in January 2006 Zayyad intends to pursue a career as a criminal lawyer. She has been vice president of the Muslim Law Student Organization, secretary of the Alternative Dispute Resolution Organization and treasurer of the Law Enforcement Society Organization at school. Source: In The Loop. The Weekly Newsletter of The John Marshall Law School

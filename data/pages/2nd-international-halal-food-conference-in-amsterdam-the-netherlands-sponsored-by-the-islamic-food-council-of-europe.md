The Islamic Food Council of Europe Announces the 2nd International Halal Food Conference in Amsterdam—Netherlands Theme: Halal Certification on Global Trade The Islamic Food Council of Europe (IFCE) is organizing a conference on Halal Food. *Venue of Conference: Sheraton Amsterdam Airport Hotel, Schiphol Boulevard 101, Amsterdam, The Netherlands *Date: Monday, April 24, 2006 *Conference Fees: €185.00 for IFCE Client Companies, €225.00 for Other Attendees This 2nd International Halal Food Conference will feature guest speakers from several Muslim countries and Europe who will discuss important issues like import regulations, halal certification programs and how they benefit domestic and international markets in a global environment. The conference is tailored for food industry professionals, academicians, students, government agencies and exporters. Lectures and discussions to be held during this conference will be beneficial and rewarding for all attendees. You and your colleagues are invited to participate in this conference.

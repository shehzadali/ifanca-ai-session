Servings: 6 Ingredients 2 pounds okra (fresh or frozen) 1 head garlic olive oil for frying okra and garlic 1 cup tomato sauce water, as needed salt, pepper, allspice to taste 2 cups rice, if desired Step 1 Slice okra into bite size pieces, discarding the top hat part. Step 2 Fry the okra in olive oil to get a golden sear, then remove the oil. Step 3 Add tomato sauce and a blend of salt, pepper, and allspice to taste. Step 4 Add just enough water to cover the okra comfortably and let simmer. Step 5 In a clean pan, warm 2 tablespoons of olive oil and fry the garlic until it develops a golden sear. Step 6 Add the garlic directly to the simmering okra. Step 7 Let the mixture simmer for another 5 minutes, then enjoy over a bed of rice.

A group of students at the Harvard Business School is conducting a research study on the zabiha halal meat retail market in the United States. An integral part of the research involves assessing consumption patterns, customer preferences, and the extent to which current zabiha halal meat retailers are providing satisfactory service. The research team has developed a short, anonymous survey that will enable them to collect this vital data. Whether you are currently a customer of zabiha halal meat or not, your participation in this survey will be of tremendous help in this study. The survey should take no more than 5 minutes to complete and can be accessed through the following web link: http://poll.hbs.edu/poll/open/pollTakerOpen.jsp?poll=108068 Jazakum Allah Khairan, Mohamed Ali Vaid & Tariq Al Sudairy

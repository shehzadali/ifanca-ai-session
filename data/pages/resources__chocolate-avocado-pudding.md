Back to All Chocolate Avocado Pudding Omolara Funmilayo June 30, 2023 Copied! Servings: 3 Time: 10 minutes Ingredients 2 avocados ½ cup pitted dates, chopped, soaked, and drained 2 tablespoons maple syrup ½ cup plain coconut milk ⅓ cup cocoa powder Step 1 Add the avocado, soaked dates, and maple syrup to a food processor and pulse to combine. Step 2 Add the coconut milk and blend until the avocado mixture is smooth, scraping down the sides of the bowl as needed. Step 3 Add the cocoa powder and blend again until combined. Step 4 Serve chilled. Enjoy! Notes One serving size is approximately ½ cup of pudding. Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 2 days. For sweeter pudding, adjust maple syrup to taste. For additional toppings, add fresh berries or toasted coconut.

Malaysia Grain Co. Ltd. is not halal certified by IFANCA. It appears that Malaysia Grain Co. Ltd. has fraudulently made amendments to an IFANCA halal certificate issued to another legitimately certified company. This is an unauthorized use of the IFANCA halal certificate. Please note that Malaysia Grain Co. Ltd. has not been certified by IFANCA and none of its products has been certified by IFANCA either. Anyone encountering this fraudulent IFANCA halal certificate is requested to advise Malaysia Grain Co. Ltd. that it is unacceptable and to report this to IFANCA by mail at IFANCA, 777 Busse Highway, Park Ridge, Illinois 60068, USA; by phone at +001.847.993.0034 by fax at +001.847.993.0038. For more information, please contact: IFANCA Tel: (847) 993-0034

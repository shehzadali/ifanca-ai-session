Back to All Watermelon and Feta Salad Saira Mohiuddin June 30, 2014 Copied! Ingredients 1 whole seedless watermelon, cubed ½ pound halal feta cheese, cubed ½ cup balsamic vinegar ⅓ cup pure cane sugar fresh mint, chopped cracked black pepper extra virgin olive oil Step 1 Prepare balsamic reduction. Heat small sauce pan over medium heat; add balsamic vinegar and sugar. Bring mixture to a quick boil, then reduce flame to low and simmer for 8 minutes. Cool and refrigerate before serving. Step 2 Plate watermelon and feta. Garnish with mint and add cracked black pepper. Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil. Step 3 Drizzle with balsamic reduction and serve immediately.

Servings: 6 | Time: 30 minutes Ingredients 400g (14 oz.) fine bulgur 2 1/2 cups hot water 2 1/2 teaspoons salt 1 onion 4 tablespoons tomato paste 4 tablespoons pepper paste 1 tablespoon cumin 1 tablespoon chili powder 1 tablespoon isot chili pepper (optional) 2 tablespoons pomegranate molasses 1/2 bunch parsley 1/2 cup olive oil Step 1 Let the mixture of hot water and fine bulgur sit for 20 minutes, until the water is absorbed and the bulgur softens. Step 2 Meanwhile, fry the finely cut onions in a pan with 1/2 cup of olive oil until they are golden. Once they are golden add the tomato and pepper paste and fry with the onions for about 2 minutes. Step 3 Add the spices, salt and pomegranate molasses to the bulgur. After the fried ingredients are cooled slightly, knead and mix thoroughly with your hands until all the ingredients are well combined. Step 4 Add finely chopped parsley and mix again. Take small portions of the mixture and shape them into traditional Çiğ Köfte by squeezing them in your palm to create ridges. Step 5 Serve with lettuce.

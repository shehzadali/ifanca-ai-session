Back to All Green Leafy Nigerian Medley Maryam Funmilayo March 31, 2022 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 1 cup extra-virgin organic coconut oil 1 cup onions, diced 1 tablespoon ground coriander 1 tablespoon ground cumin 1 tablespoon thyme 1 tablespoon sweet basil leaves 1 tablespoon curry powder 1 tablespoon rosemary 1 tablespoon cayenne pepper ½ tablespoon sea salt 5 cups organic amaranth greens ( Efo Tete ) with stems, chopped 5 cups organic cabbage, chopped 5 cups organic collards with stems, chopped 5 cups organic kale with stems, chopped 3 cups organic cilantro with stems, chopped Step 1 In a large pot, heat the coconut oil over medium-low heat. Step 2 Add the diced onions. Step 3 Steam-fry for 10 minutes. Step 4 Add the spices and steam-fry for another 10 minutes. Step 5 Add all the greens and simmer for 10 minutes until the greens are tender. Step 6 Serve hot with bulgur or brown rice.

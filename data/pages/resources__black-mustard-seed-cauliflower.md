Back to All Black Mustard Seed Cauliflower Saira Mohiuddin December 31, 2020 Copied! Servings: 4-6 Ingredients 1 tablespoon ghee ¼ cup olive oil 2 medium onions, chopped 3 garlic cloves, chopped 2½ pounds cauliflower florets Himalayan salt fresh black pepper 1 teaspoon turmeric ½ teaspoon red chili powder 7 curry leaves 1 teaspoon black mustard seeds ½ teaspoon lemon zest ½ teaspoon lemon juice ½ cup cilantro, chopped Step 1 Heat a large pot on the stove over a medium flame. Step 2 Add olive oil to the pot. Step 3 Add garlic, onions, and spices. Step 4 Cook for 2 minutes. Step 5 Add cauliflower to the pot and cover. Step 6 Cook for 8-10 minutes. Step 7 Heat a small frying pan over a medium flame and add the ghee. Step 8 Add curry leaves, black mustard seeds, and lemon zest to the pan with the ghee. Step 9 Cook for 3 minutes, then pour on top of cooked cauliflower. Step 10 Garnish with cilantro and lemon juice.

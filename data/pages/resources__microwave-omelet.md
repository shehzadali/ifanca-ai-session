Back to All Microwave Omelet Roger Othman September 30, 2018 Copied! Servings: 1 Ingredients 2 large eggs 2 tablespoons Organic Valley Lactose Free Fat Free milk ¼ orange bell pepper ½ medium tomato 1 tablespoon cooked corn or other vegetable dash of Cajun spice 2 slices rye bread 4 slices Cabot Extra Sharp Cheddar cheese Step 1 Dice ½ of the orange bell pepper. Step 2 Dice 1 slice of the tomato. Step 3 Crack eggs into microwave egg cooker and scramble them. Step 4 Add diced pepper and tomato and mix. Step 5 Add cooked corn and mix. Step 6 Add dash of Cajun spice and mix. Step 7 Place microwave egg cooker in microwave and microwave 3 minutes on high. Step 8 Carefully remove omelet from container. Be careful, the container will be hot. Step 9 Serve with remaining tomato, pepper, cheese, and bread.

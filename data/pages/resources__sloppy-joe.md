Back to All Sloppy Joe Aisha Kureishy September 30, 2015 Copied! Preparation Time: 5-7 minutes | Cooking Time: 20-25 minutes Ingredients ½ lb extra lean ground beef 1 medium finely chopped onion 1 finely chopped garlic ½ teaspoon ground black pepper salt to taste 1 green finely chopped banana pepper (remove seeds) 4-6 tablespoons of any cooking oil 4-6 hot dog buns lettuce, tomato & cucumber for garnish Step 1 Heat oil in a medium pot, add ground beef in the pot and stir. Step 2 Add onion, garlic, salt, black pepper and banana pepper. Step 3 Stir on medium heat until nice and brown. Step 4 Cover and simmer for a few minutes before removing. Step 5 Mix ketchup or BBQ sauce for a Southern touch. Step 6 Spread on hot dog buns. Add garnish for extra taste.

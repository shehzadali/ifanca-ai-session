Ingredients ½ cup ground allspice ½ cup ground cloves ⅓ cup ground fenugreek ⅓ cup ground ginger ⅓ cup ground nutmeg ¼ cup ground black pepper ¼ cup ground cinnamon Preparation Combine all the ingredients thoroughly and store in an air-tight container. Uses Used in and on numerous meat preparations.

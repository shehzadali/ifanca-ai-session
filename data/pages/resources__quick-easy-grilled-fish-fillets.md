Preparation Time: 5 minutes | Cooking Time: 20-25 minutes Ingredients 3-5 medium sized fresh tilapia fish fillets 1 lemon salt to taste black pepper to taste ¾ teaspoon garlic powder Step 1 Cut lemon and rub it on each side of the fillets. Step 2 Sprinkle and spread salt, black pepper and garlic powder evenly on fish fillets. Step 3 Put baker’s cooling rack on gas burner. Step 4 Grill each fillet side thoroughly on medium-low heat until evenly cooked. Step 5 Enjoy. Chef's Note: Beef or chicken kabobs may need meat tenderizer in order to be cooked properly and in a timely manner. Foil paper can be placed under or around the burner according to stove or cook top style for easy clean-up.

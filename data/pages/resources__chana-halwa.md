Back to All Chana Halwa Kazma Zaidi Recipe June 30, 2024 Copied! Servings: 12 Time: 30-40 minutes Ingredients 1 cup chana dal (gram flour) 1/2 cup ghee 1/4 teaspoon cardamom powder 1/2 cup sugar 1 1/2 cups milk 1/2 cup water 1 teaspoon coconut powder (for garnish) 1/4 cup chopped almonds and pistachios (for garnish) Step 1 Soak the chana dal in a bowl with water overnight. Step 2 Heat ghee in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat. Step 3 Add the chana, stirring continuously, until golden brown and fragrant for around 8-10 minutes. Step 4 In a separate pot, bring milk and water to a boil, then add sugar and stir until dissolved. Step 5 Gradually pour the liquid mixture into the roasted chana while stirring continuously to avoid lumps. Step 6 Cook on low heat for 15-20 minutes until thickened and the ghee separates. Step 7 Stir in cardamom powder and cook for an additional 5 minutes. Step 8 Remove from heat, garnish with chopped nuts and/or coconut powder, and serve warm. For square pieces, transfer the halwa to a flat tray, pat it down with a spoon while it's still warm, and use a knife to slice it into squares.

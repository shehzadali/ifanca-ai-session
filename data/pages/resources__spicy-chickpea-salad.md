Back to All Spicy Chickpea Salad Jacob Taylor December 31, 2020 Copied! Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 can (29 ounces) of chickpeas, rinsed ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice ¾ teaspoon coarse salt ½ teaspoon black pepper 1½ teaspoons cayenne pepper 2 jalapeño peppers, finely sliced or chopped (increase or decrease the amount based on your preference) 3 carrots, peeled and shredded ½ cup pitted Kalamata olives 1 cup baby arugula, chopped Step 1 Microwave chickpeas in a medium bowl on high for 1½ minutes or until hot. Step 2 Stir in oil, juice, salt, black pepper, and cayenne pepper and let sit for 30 minutes. Step 3 Add carrots, olives, and jalapeños. Once the chickpeas have reached room temperature, add the arugula. Step 4 Toss the salad well and then serve. Enjoy!

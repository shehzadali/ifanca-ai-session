Back to All Chocolate Hazelnut Mixed Berry Smoothie YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD June 30, 2015 Copied! Servings: 2 Ingredients 1½ cups Organic Valley® Organic Lowfat 1% Milk 2 small bananas 4 tablespoons chocolate hazelnut spread ½ cup frozen strawberries ½ cup blueberries ½ cup blackberries ½ cup raspberries Step 1 Place all ingredients in blender and process until smooth. Step 2 Serve in a glass with a straw and a few peppermint leaves for garnish (optional).

Back to All What is the policy for maintaining, extending, and renewing the halal certificate? Salman Chaudry March 16, 2023 Copied! To maintain the halal certificate, the client must continue complying with the halal requirements. IFANCA may conduct surveillance audits to ensure that the facility continues to meet the halal requirements. To extend the certification scope, the client must submit a request to IFANCA. Evaluation and approval decision is required before extending any certificate. If necessary, IFANCA may conduct a surveillance audit before the approval decision. For certification renewal, please submit a request to halal@ifanca.org .

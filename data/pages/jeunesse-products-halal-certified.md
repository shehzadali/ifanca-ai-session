In our August 20, 2015, announcement concerning Jeunesse products, we stated that “all Jeunesse products displaying the IFANCA Crescent-M logo on the label meet our halal certification requirements and are considered to be halal.” The products are halal certified by IFANCA when displaying the IFANCA Crescent-M halal certification mark on the label.​​

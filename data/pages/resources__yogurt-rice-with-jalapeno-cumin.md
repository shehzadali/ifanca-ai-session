Back to All Yogurt Rice with Jalapeño & Cumin Chef Demetrios Haralambatos, Executive Chef of Kontos Foods Inc. September 30, 2021 Copied! Ingredients 4 cups water 2 cups white rice 1 tablespoon canola oil 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced 1 teaspoon whole cumin seeds 1 jalapeño, thinly sliced 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped ¾ teaspoon salt 1 container (6 ounces) halal unflavored low-fat yogurt (Do not use Greek yogurt, as it is too thick. Use only low-fat yogurt.) Step 1 Bring the water to a boil. Step 2 Stir the rice into the boiling water, bring to a boil, and cover. Then lower heat and simmer for 20 minutes. Step 3 When the rice is ready, place a large skillet over medium-high heat and add canola oil. Step 4 When the oil is hot, stir in the ginger, cumin, and jalapeño. Sauté for 30 seconds, then lower the heat on the skillet. Step 5 Stir in the rice, cilantro, salt, and yogurt. Combine and cook for 1 minute. Step 6 Serve the rice with roasted halal chicken, grilled salmon, or grilled halal lamb chops.

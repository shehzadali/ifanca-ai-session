Back to All Shrimp and Vegetable Stir Fry Sakina Syeda December 31, 2018 Copied! Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 pound fresh or frozen large uncooked shrimp, peeled and deveined 1 zucchini, washed and julienned 1 yellow squash, washed and julienned 1 red onion, sliced crosswise ½ pound fresh or frozen broccoli, cut into small chunks 3-4 garlic cloves, finely chopped 3-4 teaspoons olive oil fresh black pepper and salt to taste 1-2 teaspoons soy sauce 1 dozen mushrooms, sliced 3 carrots, peeled and julienned 2-3 green onions, 1 inch sliced salt and pepper to taste Step 1 Heat 1 teaspoon of olive oil in a medium size cast iron skillet. Step 2 Add garlic, shrimp, salt, and fresh pepper to the skillet. Step 3 Cook until shrimp turns pink. Step 4 Remove shrimp from the skillet and add 1-2 teaspoons of olive oil in the skillet. Step 5 Add onions, zucchini, yellow squash, and broccoli to the skillet. Step 6 Add in the soy sauce. Step 7 Cook for about 5 to 7 minutes. (Do not over cook your veggies). Step 8 Once veggies are tender, add the shrimp back to the skillet. Step 9 Add green onions into the mix. Step 10 Cook for another 1-2 minutes. Step 11 Mix the stir fry well to coat with the seasonings. Step 12 Plate stir fry and garnish with green onions. Step 13 Serve immediately.

Back to All Baked Sweet Potatoes with Sliced Apples and Figs YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD December 31, 2015 Copied! Servings: 2 Ingredients 2 large sweet potatoes, washed, peeled, and chopped 1 cup figs, sliced 1 large honey crisp apple, sliced into thin wedges 1 cup water 2 tablespoons butter, divided 1 tablespoon cinnamon ½ cup brown sugar (optional) Step 1 Preheat oven to 375°F. Step 2 Lightly coat a medium-sized baking dish with 1 tablespoon of butter. Step 3 Place sweet potatoes, figs, and apple slices into buttered baking dish; add water. Step 4 Top with cinnamon, remaining tablespoon of butter, and brown sugar if desired. Step 5 Bake for 45 minutes until sweet potatoes are soft and golden brown.

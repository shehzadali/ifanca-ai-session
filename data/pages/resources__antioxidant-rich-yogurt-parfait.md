Back to All Antioxidant-Rich Yogurt Parfait Omolara Funmilayo Recipe March 31, 2026 Copied! Servings: 2 Time: 20 minutes Ingredients 1 cup plain Greek yogurt 2 teaspoons chia seeds 1 cup mixed berries 2 tablespoons coconut flakes 2 tablespoons hemp seeds 1 tablespoon ground flaxseeds (optional) 2 teaspoons honey 2 small sprigs fresh mint Step 1 In a small bowl, soak the chia seeds in 3 teaspoons of water for about 10 minutes, until gel-like. Step 2 Add 1/4 cup of plain Greek yogurt in each glass for the first layer. Step 3 Spoon half of the chia gel into each glass, for the second layer. Step 4 Divide half of the coconut flakes, hemp seeds, flaxseeds (if using) and berries between both glasses, for the third layer. Step 5 Repeat another yogurt layer, chia gel, coconut flakes, hemp seeds, flaxseeds then remaining berries. Step 6 Finally, drizzle each cup with 1 tsp honey, and garnish with a mint sprig. Serve and enjoy! Notes: Refrigerate leftovers in airtight container for up to three days.
Use crushed nuts for extra toppings.
Use coconut yogurt for a dairy free option.

Chef Bombay Brand Samosas, produced by Aliya’s Foods, Sherwood Park, Alberta, Canada are not certified halal by the Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America (IFANCA). The Crescent-M logo is a registered trademark of IFANCA. The use of the Crescent-M logo or any markings of IFANCA by Aliya’s Food is an unauthorized use. Retailers are advised to remove from their shelves all Chef Bombay Brand products displaying the Crescent-M logo and/or IFANCA markings.

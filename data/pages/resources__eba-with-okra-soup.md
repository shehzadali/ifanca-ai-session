Back to All Eba with Okra Soup Maryam Funmilayo June 30, 2022 Copied! Servings: 6 Ingredients 3 cups garri (dried cassava flour) 5 cups water 2 cups okra, blended 2 cups halal chicken or beef broth 1 teaspoon italian seasoning 1 tablespoon curry powder 3 teaspoons cayenne pepper ½ cup iru (locust beans) 1 cup dried stockfish Step 1 Boil the water. Step 2 Pour the garri into the boiling water and stir consistently with a wooden spoon or spatula until there is a fine dough. This dough is known as eba . Step 3 Set the eba aside in a separate bowl and cover to keep it hot. Step 4 Add the blended okra, seasoning, curry powder, cayenne pepper, iru, and dried stockfish into the broth and cook for 10 minutes. Step 5 Dish out the eba and okra soup onto separate plates. Step 6 Enjoy eating with your (clean) bare hands.

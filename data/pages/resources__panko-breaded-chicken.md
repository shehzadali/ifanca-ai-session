Back to All Panko Breaded Chicken Imran Ikram March 31, 2018 Copied! Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 pound boneless halal chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch strips, pat dry 2 teaspoons salt 1 teaspoon garlic powder 1 teaspoon onion powder 1 teaspoon smoked paprika ½ teaspoon chili powder (optional) 1 cup flour 2 eggs 2 cups panko bread crumbs Step 1 Preheat oven to 425°F. Step 2 Combine the seasonings in a small bowl and liberally season both sides of the chicken. Step 3 In three separate, large bowls, lay out the flour, egg, and panko from left to right. Step 4 Dredge the chicken pieces in flour, thoroughly coat in egg, and coat with the panko. Set aside. Step 5 Grease a cooling rack with cooking spray and place over a baking tray. This is optional, but it allows circulation and prevents sogginess. Place the coated chicken on the rack. If you do not have a rack, just place directly on a pan and flip once during cooking. Step 6 Bake for 20-30 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through.

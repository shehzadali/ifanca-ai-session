We are proud to announce that IFANCA has received HAK (Halal Accreditation Body) accreditation. This achievement reinforces our dedication to providing top-quality halal certification services and highlights our commitment to upholding the highest industry standards. The HAK accreditation enhances our credibility, enabling us to better serve businesses worldwide and ensuring that consumers can trust our certification process. Thank you for your support as we continue our mission of excellence in halal certification.

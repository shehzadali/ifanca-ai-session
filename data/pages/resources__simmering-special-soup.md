Back to All Simmering Special Soup Sarah Ibrahim September 30, 2013 Copied! Ingredients 2 cups of any type of dried split peas, washed and soaked for at least 2-3 hours 1 large onion 1 medium potato, peeled and cut into small cubes 2 clove garlic, minced 2 medium celery stalks, chopped 2 medium carrots, peeled and chopped into cubes 8 cups water or vegetable/chicken broth ½ teaspoon ground pepper pinch of salt according to your taste 3-4 small pieces of chicken with bones (optional) Step 1 Heat peas and water to boiling in big 5-6 quart pot. Step 2 Add all other ingredients except carrots. Step 3 Once water boils, lower the heat, cover and cook for 3-5 hours at low temperature. Step 4 Remove bones from chicken, add chicken if desired and carrots, cover and simmer for about 30 minutes or until soup is at desired consistency. Step 5 Enjoy with garlic bread or by itself.

WASHINGTON, D.C.-March 4, 2026 – The Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America (IFANCA®) was a proud sponsor of the National U.S.–Arab Chamber of Commerce (NUSACC) 2026 Annual Iftar, held at the Ritz-Carlton in Washington, D.C. The event was hosted in honor of the League of Arab States and the Arab diplomatic corps and brought together distinguished leaders from government, diplomacy, business, and faith communities. Now in its 15th year, NUSACC’s Annual Iftar is widely regarded as one of the most prominent Ramadan gatherings in the United States. The event convenes senior U.S. government officials, ambassadors representing the Arab diplomatic corps, business executives, and community leaders, creating a unique platform where policy, trade, culture, and diplomacy intersect. This distinguished gathering reflects an ongoing commitment to strengthening partnerships and engagement across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region. By supporting this event, IFANCA reaffirmed its dedication to fostering global collaboration, supporting the growth of the halal industry, and promoting trust and transparency in halal certification worldwide. Founded in 1982, IFANCA® is a globally recognized halal certification body dedicated to serving consumers and industry by ensuring the integrity of halal products. Through partnerships with governments, businesses, and international organizations, IFANCA continues to support the growth of the global halal market and facilitates trade across regions. The National U.S.–Arab Chamber of Commerce (NUSACC), now celebrating its 50th anniversary, serves as America’s premier commercial gateway to the Middle East and North Africa. Officially recognized by the League of Arab States and the Union of Arab Chambers, NUSACC plays a key role in strengthening U.S.–Arab commercial relations and promoting bilateral trade and investment. IFANCA is honored to support initiatives that bring together leaders from across sectors and regions, especially during the blessed month of Ramadan, a time that emphasizes community, generosity, and shared values.

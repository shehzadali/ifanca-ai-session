Malaysia International Halal Showcase 2005 (MIHAS 2005) has been scheduled for 28-31 July 2005. This is an opportunity for companies to showcase their Halal certified products and to interact with other Halal certified product providers. If you are currently a certified Halal product provider or are interested in becoming one, you won’t want to miss this. Come and meet IFANCA and the other participants in Kuala Lumpur. For more information, go to http://www.mihas.com.my/ . Halal is recognized as a new benchmark for safety and quality assurance. Halal certified foods are highly sought by consumers looking for clean and pure food products. That includes the 1.5 billion Muslims around the world as well as others from diverse races and religions.

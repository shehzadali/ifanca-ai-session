Prep Time: 3 hours Cook Time: 1 hour Servings: 4-6 Ingredients 5 cups all-purpose flour 1 cup whole wheat flour 1 cup ground flax seed, wheat germ, wheat bran 1½ tablespoons yeast ½ tablespoon salt pinch sugar water, enough for kneading Step 1 Mix all the ingredients together and knead until dough is no longer sticky. Step 2 Cover the dough with a lid. Step 3 Let it sit for 2 hours, so it can rise to double its size. Step 4 Knead it down again. Step 5 Let dough rise again to double. Step 6 Preheat oven to 500°F. Step 7 Roll out dough into palm-sized balls. Step 8 Oil 2 9×13-inch baking pans (you may need more depending on size of your dough balls). Step 9 Brush oil lightly over dough. Step 10 Let it rise again for 30 to 40 minutes. Step 11 Bake dough balls at 500°F for 10 minutes. Step 12 Reduce oven temperature to 350°F and bake bread for another 50 minutes. Serve hot. For leftovers, let bread cool completely and place in sealed plastic.

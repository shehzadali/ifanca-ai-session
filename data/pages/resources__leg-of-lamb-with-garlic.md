Back to All Leg of Lamb with Garlic Chef Demetrios Haralambatos September 30, 2020 Copied! Ingredients 1 leg of halal lamb (about 7 to 8 pounds) 2 garlic cloves, in slivers, sliced lengthwise 2 lemons, cut in half 4 additional garlic cloves, minced salt fresh ground pepper oregano, crushed about ½ cup water potatoes, sliced lengthwise (optional) onions, sliced lengthwise (optional) Step 1 Pre-heat oven to 350°F. Step 2 Insert the tip of a paring knife in various locations around the leg of lamb. Step 3 Insert the slivers of garlic into the pierced locations. Step 4 Squeeze and rub the lemons all over the lamb, then place the lemon in the bottom of the roasting pan. Step 5 Rub the lamb with minced garlic. Step 6 Place in the roasting pan and add enough water to cover the bottom of the pan. Step 7 Bake in the center of the oven for 2½ hours until done. In the last 30 minutes of baking, you can add potatoes and onions to the liquid in the roasting pan. Step 8 Remove from oven and allow to sit for 20 to 30 minutes before slicing.

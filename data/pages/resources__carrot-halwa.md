Back to All Carrot Halwa Jawairia Sial Ashraf June 30, 2013 Copied! Serving Size: 10 | Total Prep Time: 4 hours and 15 minutes Ingredients 2½ pounds shredded carrots 5 cardamom 2 cups milk ⅓ cup sugar 3 tablespoons ghee 1 tablespoon chopped almonds for garnish Step 1 Mix carrots, cardamom and milk and cook on low heat for about 4 hours. Step 2 Once the liquid has dried up, add sugar and ghee and fry for 10-15 minutes. Step 3 Garnish with almonds and serve warm.

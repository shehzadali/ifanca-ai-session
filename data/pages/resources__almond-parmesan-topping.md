Copyright © 2016 Roni Enten Vissoker Ingredients ½ cup ground almonds/almond flour 1 teaspoon garlic powder ½ teaspoon turmeric ½ teaspoon sweet paprika ½ teaspoon sea salt Step 1 Combine all ingredients. Step 2 Shake it up and sprinkle on rice, pasta, toast, steamed veggies, and more. (Note: You can change up spice content to suit your taste.)

Effective October 18, 2007, Ningbo Zhenhai (Haide) Industry Co. Ltd., China is no longer halal certified by IFANCA. This action is a result of the company’s non-compliance with halal guidelines. If you have any questions please contact the IFANCA Halal Helpline at: +1-877-HALAL23 or by email at Contact Us .

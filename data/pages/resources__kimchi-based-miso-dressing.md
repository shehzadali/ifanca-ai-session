Back to All Kimchi-Based Miso Dressing YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD March 31, 2021 Copied! Servings: 16 (Serving size is 2 tablespoons) Ingredients 4 tablespoons white miso 6 tablespoons kimchi, chopped ½ cup vegetable oil ½ cup extra soft tofu 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar flake salt vegetable broth (optional) Step 1 Combine kimchi, miso, tofu, oil, and vinegar in a food processor, and purée until smooth. Step 2 Add salt. Step 3 Refrigerate until ready to use. The dressing will thicken as it stands. Step 4 Prior to serving, thin with vegetable broth as needed. Note: You can also make the dressing up to four days in advance. Keep it stored in an airtight container and chilled.

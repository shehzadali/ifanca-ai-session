Back to All Carrot Rice Pudding Jawairia Sial Ashraf June 30, 2013 Copied! Serving Size: 25 | Total Prep Time: 4 hours Ingredients 2½ pounds shredded carrots 5 cardamom 1 cup rice 14 cups water 5 cups milk 1 cup sugar 2 tablespoons ghee almonds for garnish Step 1 Combine carrots, cardamom, rice and water and cook on medium heat for about 3 hours until the rice starts breaking. Step 2 Add milk and bring to a boil. Step 3 Add ghee and keep mixing. Cook on medium/high heat until the mixture reaches the desired consistency. Step 4 Add sugar and reduce the liquid until the mixture reaches the desired consistency. Step 5 Garnish with almonds.

IFANCA is pleased to announce our renewed partnership with Saudi International Halal Expo & Summit 2023 . This Expo is the world’s largest halal exhibition in the MENA region. Location: Riyadh International Convention & Exhibition Center in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia Dates: November 19 to November 21, 2023 Organized by 1st Arabia, the Expo offers a platform to promote your brand to this important economic region. We invite you to attend the Expo and take advantage of the opportunity to showcase your products and services. Saudi Arabia has demonstrated its continued interest in halal. The Saudi halal food market is projected to grow at a CAGR of over 9% by the year 2024. At last year’s Expo, the organizers hosted 90 exhibitors from around the world and welcomed 4,500 visitors from 28 countries. Their plans for this year’s Expo are even larger: the hosts anticipate more than 200 exhibitors and 10,000 visitors from more than 35 countries. Click here for more information. If you would like to register, please contact Dr. Saeed Hayek at saeed.hayek@ifanca.org .

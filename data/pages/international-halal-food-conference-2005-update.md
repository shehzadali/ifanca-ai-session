The Islamic Food and Nutrition Council of America Announces International Halal Food Conference 2005 “A WINDOW TO A HALAL WORLD” Organized by: SANHA and IFANCA Theme: Halal – A Symbol of Quality and Purity The Islamic Food And Nutrition Council Of America (IFANCA) is co-sponsoring a conference on Halal Food in conjunction with the 1st International Halal Food Conference by the South African National Halaal Authority (SANHA). * Venue of Conference: The Coast of Dreams, Durban Exhibition Centre (next to Hilton Durban), 11 Walnut Road, Durban, South Africa 4001 * Conference HQ Hotel: Hilton Durban, 12/14 Walnut Road, Durban, South Africa 4001 * Dates: Thursday, September 8, 2005 – Friday, September 9, 2005 * Conference Fees: US$295.00 (ZAR 1,995.00) This International Halal Food Conference will feature guest speakers from several Muslim countries, the USA, Europe, as well as South Africa who will discuss important issues like import regulations, halal certification programs and how they benefit domestic and international markets in a global environment. The main focus of the conference will be on halal as a symbol of quality and purity. The conference is tailored for food industry professionals, academicians, students, government agencies and exporters. Lectures and discussions to be held during this conference will be beneficial and rewarding for all attendees. You and your colleagues are invited to participate in this conference. Please indicate your willingness to participate by fax or email.

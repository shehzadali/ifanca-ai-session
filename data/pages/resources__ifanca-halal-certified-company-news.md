IFANCA Client, Nestle, Is “Role Model” for Islamic Branding, Reports WARC News As reported by WARC News, “Nestle is the leading multinational company for developing marketing strategies for Muslim consumers.” WARC is a leading supplier of information, knowledge, insight and data to the global marketing, advertising, media and research communities. Miles Young, CEO of Ogilvy & Mather, a keynote speaker at the Oxford Global Islamic Branding and Marketing Forum at the Said Business School, Oxford University, acknowledged Nestle as  having recognized Muslim shoppers as a significant market. “Nestle is probably the role model for doing this properly. It has done it out of conviction – there’s a commitment in everything the company does. ” He added that “503 of the potential audience in the so-called “next 11″ emerging economies are Muslim.” However, he pointed out that just using religious connotations as a badge without understanding what it means to be a brand could be a recipe for failure. For instance, he said, Mecca Cola had been targeted to Muslim consumers without success. Brand owners must think “seriously about how they are perceived by Muslim customers.” Olympia Food Industries Sells Halal Gyros Like Hot Cakes Chicago is home to all five of the major gyro manufacturers in the USA, including IFANCA Halal Certified Olympia Food Industries, makers of gyros, Mediterranean pastries, pita breads and various other food items for distribution to restaurants and supermarkets. Bruschetta & Toasted Flatbread Bruschetta Ingredients: red and yellow grape tomatoes, diced onion, finely diced fresh basil leaves, chopped garlic, finely chopped vegetable oil black pepper (optional) balsamic glaze* (optional) Flatbread Ingredients: Kontos Flatbread Butter, softened or olive oil Fresh herbs , finely chopped (basil, parsley, oregano, or rosemary) Bruschetta Directions: Combine tomato, onion, garlic, and basil with a little oil. If preparing in advance, refrigerate in covered container until ready to serve. Serve with prepared crispy flatbread wedges (see below). Toasted Flatbread Wedges Directions: Preheat oven to 375°F. Brush Kontos Flatbread with butter or oil. Place on a baking sheet and sprinkle with herbs. Heat in oven for about 2 minutes (or longer if you prefer them crispier). Cut into 1 to 1 ½ inch strips or wedges and place in serving basket. To add a spicy zip, sprinkle flatbread with red pepper flakes, white or black sesame seeds, and/or cracked black pepper. *Balsamic Glaze: 1 cup Balsamic vinegar, ¼ cup water, ¼ cup brown sugar. Combine and reduce by 50% over medium heat while stirring constantly. Quick & Easy Pizza Ingredients : Kontos Pizza Parlor Crust pizza sauce (or spaghetti sauce with dried oregano added) mozzarella and cheddar cheese, shredded suggested additional toppings: Fresh basil, artichoke hearts, pepperoni, crumbled sausage, and/or sliced mushrooms, peppers, onions, and olives Directions: Preheat oven to 375°F. Spread sauce over Kontos Flatbread almost to edge. Sprinkle cheese over sauce and add your favorite toppings. Bake on a baking sheet until cheese melts (about 4 minutes). For a crisper pizza crust, place directly on oven rack. Slice into wedges and serve.

Back to All Egg Sandwich Aisha Kureishy September 30, 2013 Copied! Preparation Time: 5 minutes | Cooking Time: 20-25 minutes Ingredients 4-6 organic eggs 4-6 tablespoons mayo your favorite bread tomato, lettuce & cucumber for garnish Step 1 Boil eggs for twenty to twenty-five minutes. Step 2 Rinse eggs under cold water to remove shell easily. Step 3 Put peeled hard-boiled eggs in a mixing bowl. Step 4 Add mayo and mash eggs until this mix is nice and smooth. Step 5 Add salt and black pepper to taste. Step 6 Spread lavishly on the bread, add garnish and serve

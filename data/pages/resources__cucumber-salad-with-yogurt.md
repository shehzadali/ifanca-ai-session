Back to All Cucumber Salad with Yogurt Chef Demetrios Haralambatos September 30, 2020 Copied! Ingredients 2 or 3 cucumbers 1 cup tzatziki sauce salt and pepper to taste 1 small onion, sliced in thin rings sprigs of dill Step 1 Peel the cucumbers, then slice into rings. Step 2 Place the sliced cucumbers in a bowl and add the tzatziki sauce. Step 3 Mix well and season to taste. Step 4 Place on a decorative platter. Step 5 Place the onion rings on top. Step 6 Garnish with dill. Uses Serve warm over fish, meat, or vegetables. Allow to cool and serve over vegetables.

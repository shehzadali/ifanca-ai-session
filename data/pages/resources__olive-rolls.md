Back to All Olive Rolls Chef Demetrios Haralambatos September 30, 2020 Copied! CAUTION: May contain pit fragments. Ingredients 2 cups all-purpose flour 1 teaspoon baking powder 4 teaspoons vegetable oil 1 cup halal yogurt 1 egg, beaten (for brushing) 1 cup pitted Kalamata olives, chopped 1 onion, chopped 1 teaspoon dry mint, crumbled 1 tablespoon fresh mint, chopped 1 cup fresh coriander, finely chopped Step 1 Combine flour, baking powder, and oil. Step 2 Add yogurt and mix into a pliable dough. Step 3 Set aside and allow to rest for 10 minutes. Step 4 Combine the filling ingredients, mix well, and set aside. Step 5 Pre-heat oven to 350°F. Step 6 Cut dough into 4 equal pieces, then roll out each piece to about ¾-inch thickness. Step 7 Spread ¼ of the filling over each piece and roll each as if a jelly roll. Step 8 Place on a baking sheet and brush each piece with the beaten egg. Step 9 Place baking sheet in oven. Step 10 Bake for 45 minutes: the first 30 minutes at 350°F or until golden, then at 300°F for the final 15 minutes. Variations For a more colorful presentation, substitute pimento-stuffed green olives and black olives for the Kalamata olives. As an alternative, slice each loaf into roulades or pinwheels after baking and cooling.

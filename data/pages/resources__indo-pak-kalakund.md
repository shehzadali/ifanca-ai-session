Back to All Indo-Pak Kalakund Aisha Kureishy March 31, 2012 Copied! Servings: 20-25 pcs | Prep Time: 15 min | Cooking Time: 15 min Ingredients 1 lb halal Paneer (cheese available at ethnic stores) 1 small can condensed milk 2 tbsp milk powder 3-4 cardamom (powdered) 1 tablespoon cooking oil finely chopped nuts 1 silver edible leaf (optional) Equipment 1 mixing bowl 1 food masher 1 medium saucepan 1 spatula 1 large serving platter measuring spoons Directions Wash your hands with soap and water, and have your kitchen gear ready to start making this delicious treat. Mash paneer (cheese) coarsely and add milk powder to it. Add condense milk and mix well. With an adult heat oil in the saucepan and add cardamom powder in it. Add mixture to the saucepan slowly and stir. Cook on medium heat while constantly stirring until mixture becomes thick. Let an adult remove from heat and spread onto a greased platter. Top it with silver edible leaf or with chopped nuts. Cool and cut into desired shapes.

Back to All Nargisi Koftay Aasia Farooqi Recipe June 30, 2024 Copied! Servings: 4-6 people Time: 30 minutes Ingredients ¼ cup chana dal (split gram) 1 tablespoon poppy seeds 2 teaspoons coriander seeds 1 teaspoon cumin seeds 1 lb halal mincemeat (mutton, chicken, or beef) ½ cup cilantro, chopped 4-5 green chilies, chopped 1 medium-sized onion, chopped 1 teaspoon red chili powder 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper 1 teaspoon coriander powder 1 teaspoon cumin powder ½ teaspoon garam masala ½ teaspoon turmeric powder 1 teaspoon ginger paste 1 teaspoon garlic paste 2 tablespoons ghee 1 teaspoon salt 6 boiiled eggs, peeled (whole or cut in half) For the Curry: ⅓ cup oil 2 bay leaves 4 green cardamom pods, crushed 4-5 cloves, crushed 1 teaspoon ginger paste 1 teaspoon garlic paste 1 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon red chili powder 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper ½ teaspoon garam masala 1 tablespoon coriander powder 1 teaspoon cumin powder ¼ cup fried onion ½ cup yogurt Step 1 In a pan, roast the chana dal, poppy seeds, coriander seeds, and cumin seeds until golden brown. Let them cool, then grind into a fine powder. Step 2 In a food processor, combine the mincemeat, chopped cilantro, green chilies, onion, and all spices (red chili powder, crushed red pepper, coriander powder, cumin powder, garam masala, turmeric powder), ginger paste, garlic paste, ghee, and salt. Blend until smooth. Step 3 Take the boiled eggs (whole or halved) and wrap the spiced mincemeat mixture around them to form koftay. Step 4 In a deep frying pan, heat oil over medium heat. Fry the koftay until golden brown on all sides. Remove and set aside. Step 5 In the same oil, add bay leaves, crushed cardamom, and crushed cloves. Sauté briefly until aromatic. Add ginger-garlic pastes, salt, red chili powders, garam masala, coriander powder, and cumin powder. Fry the spices for a few minutes, adding a little water to prevent burning. Step 6 Place the fried koftay into the curry sauce and simmer for about 5 minutes. Then, add ¼ cup water and let it cook for another 5 minutes. Step 7 Blend the fried onions and yogurt together, then add this mixture to the curry. Cover and cook for another 10 minutes on low to medium heat until the sauce thickens and flavors meld together. Step 8 Garnish with cilantro and serve hot with naan or rice.

Back to All Vegan Chili Samer Abbas December 31, 2013 Copied! Ingredients ⅓ cup dry beans (mix of black, pinto, and kidney) ⅓ cup onion (yellow, white, or red), chopped 4 cloves garlic 4 shitake mushrooms 4 cherry tomatoes 1 cup crushed tomatoes 6 ounces non-GMO vegan soy chorizo dash each of the following: cumin, turmeric, chili powder, cayenne pepper, paprika, red curry powder, garlic powder, ginger, nutritional yeast Step 1 Soak dried beans in water overnight. Step 2 The next day, place drained beans in a crockpot. Add remaining ingredients. Step 3 Cook on low setting for at least 5 hours. Stir every hour, if possible.

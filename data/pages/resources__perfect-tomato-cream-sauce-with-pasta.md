Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 small onion, diced 1 tablespoon olive oil 1 clove garlic, minced 33.5 ounces tomato sauce 1 teaspoon salt ½ teaspoon black pepper 1 teaspoon dried basil 2 tablespoons Organic Valley® Cream Cheese OR Tofutti® Better Than Cream Cheese (Vegan) 1 pound cooked pasta (fettucine, penne, rigatoni, whatever you like!) Step 1 Heat oil over medium heat. Sauté onions until soft, about 5 minutes. Step 2 Add garlic and sauté for about 30 seconds. Step 3 Add tomato sauce and bring to a boil. Step 4 Stir in salt, pepper, and basil. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 10 minutes. Step 5 Whisk in cream cheese well and heat for a couple more minutes, until heated through and the sauce becomes a nice orange color. Step 6 Add cooked pasta to sauce and toss gently to coat. You can also add sautéed shrimp or seasoned halal chicken to this dish. Sautéed mushrooms tossed in is a great way to keep it vegetarian-friendly while making it a more substantial meal.

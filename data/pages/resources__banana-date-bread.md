Back to All Banana Date Bread Sofia Alam September 30, 2013 Copied! Preparation Time: 10 minutes | Cooking Time: 15-20 minutes Ingredients 1 cup flour 1 teaspoon baking soda ½ teaspoon salt 2 eggs 1 stick of butter, melted 2 overripe bananas, mashed or chopped up 5-6 Medjool pitted dates chopped up in small pieces ½ cup of chopped walnuts Step 1 Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Step 2 Grease 9 x 5 loaf pan. Step 3 Mix all the ingredients well, pour into the pan and bake for 40 minutes or until top is brown. Step 4 Best way to know if it’s done is insert toothpick in middle, it comes out clean, bread is done. Step 5 Slice it, and serve it warm.

Back to All Air Fryer “Roasted” Chicken Husna T. Ghani September 30, 2022 Copied! Ingredients 1 whole halal chicken, cut into pieces 1 teaspoon garlic paste 1 teaspoon ginger paste ½ teaspoon turmeric ½ teaspoon red chili powder 1 teaspoon Himalayan salt 2 teaspoons halal dry steak seasoning 2 teaspoons rosemary leaves, chopped 1 teaspoon olive oil cooking spray to lightly coat air fryer Step 1 Mix chicken pieces with garlic, ginger, turmeric, chili powder, salt, steak seasoning, and olive oil. Coat well. Step 2 Mix in rosemary leaves. Step 3 Lightly coat the inside of the air fryer with cooking spray and place the chicken inside in a single layer. Step 4 Follow instructions for the air fryer (usually there is a chicken setting).

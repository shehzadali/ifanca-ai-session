Back to All Olive & Cheese Bruschetta YaQutullah Ibraheem Muhammad, MS, RDN, LD December 31, 2014 Copied! Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 cup plum tomatoes, diced and seeded ¼ cup chopped green olives ¼ cup chopped black olives 2 tablespoons fresh basil, chopped ⅛ teaspoon salt ⅛ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper 1 clove garlic, peeled and crushed 16 (¼-inch thick) slices French bread baguette 1 cup Cabot Seriously Sharp Cheddar cheese, shredded Step 1 Preheat oven to 400° F. Step 2 Combine first 7 ingredients in a small bowl and set aside. Step 3 Arrange French bread slices on a baking sheet. Step 4 Bake until lightly browned and crisp. Step 5 Remove toast slices from oven and cool 2 minutes. Step 6 Sprinkle cheese thinly on 1 side of each toast and top with the olive mixture.

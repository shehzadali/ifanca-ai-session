Back to All Yogurt and Honey Chef Demetrios Haralambatos September 30, 2020 Copied! In Greece, most dinners end with a refreshing bowl of yogurt. Ingredients halal yogurt honey Step 1 The yogurt must be very thick with the consistency of Greek yogurt or labneh . If it is not, place a piece of cheesecloth (available at the supermarket) in a strainer, add the yogurt, and set in the refrigerator for several hours to release its whey (water). Step 2 Spoon the yogurt into a bowl and drizzle honey over the top. Variation Add chopped walnuts.
